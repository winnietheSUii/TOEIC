# 📌 Agreement — Nouns & Pronouns / Possessive Adjectives
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 02 — Pronouns | Part 6 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Number, Gender & Person Agreement*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> A pronoun or possessive adjective must **agree** with the noun it refers to in:
> 1. **Number** — singular or plural
> 2. **Gender** — masculine, feminine, or neuter
> 3. **Person** — 1st, 2nd, or 3rd

> TOEIC places the antecedent (the noun being referred to) early in the sentence and tests whether you can identify the correct pronoun form much later.

---

## 📋 Agreement Map

| Antecedent | Pronoun/Possessive Adjective |
|------------|------------------------------|
| Singular noun (thing/organization) | `it` / `its` |
| Singular male person | `he` / `him` / `his` |
| Singular female person | `she` / `her` / `hers` |
| Plural noun or people | `they` / `them` / `their` / `theirs` |
| Compound subject (A and B) | `they` / `them` / `their` |
| Organization / company / committee | `it` / `its` (neuter, singular) |

---

## 📂 Rule 1 — Number Agreement

> Singular antecedent → singular pronoun
> Plural antecedent → plural pronoun

### ❌ Common Errors

> *"Businesses should consider the social implications of **its** activities."*
> ❌ `Businesses` = plural → ✅ `their`

> *"Mr. Pak and the other employees tried to finish it by **himself**."*
> ❌ Compound subject = plural → ✅ `themselves`

> *"A grandparent's **job** is easier than **these** of a parent."*
> ❌ `job` = singular → ✅ `that` (singular demonstrative)

---

## 📂 Rule 2 — Gender & Person Agreement

> The pronoun must match the **gender and person** of the noun it replaces.

> ✅ *"Mr. Jones enjoyed the work so much that it changed **his** career."*
> → `his` correctly refers to Mr. Jones (male)

> ✅ *"Mrs. Pringle and **her** husband attended an investment club."*
> → `her` correctly refers to Mrs. Pringle (female)

> ❌ *"The firm wishes to announce the addition of Mr. Hopkins to **his** board of directors."*
> → The firm = organization (neuter) → ✅ `its` board

> ❌ *"The country has become one of the largest producers by increasing **new** production."*
> → Should refer back to the country → ✅ `its` production

---

## 🔑 Key Rule — Organizations & Companies = `it/its`

> Even if a company has many people, it is treated as a **singular, neuter** entity.
> → Always `it` / `its`, never `they` / `their` for organizations.

> ✅ *"The library has **its** own collection of rare books."*
> ✅ *"The committee decided that the task force would not have **its** own decision-making capacity."*

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Plural noun → `its`:** `Businesses/Companies/Employees` = plural → need `their`, not `its`
- **Trap 2 — Compound subject → `himself`:** "A and B tried by **himself**" ❌ → `themselves`
- **Trap 3 — Organization → `their`:** Company/Library/Committee = singular → `its`, not `their`
- **Trap 4 — Singular demonstrative mismatch:** `job` (singular) replaced by `these` (plural) ❌ → `that`

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the blank — it's a pronoun or possessive adjective.
> **Step 2:** Hunt for the **antecedent** (the noun it refers back to) earlier in the sentence.
> **Step 3:** Check the antecedent's **number**: singular or plural?
> **Step 4:** Check its **type**: person (male/female) or thing/org (neuter)?
> **Step 5:** Match: singular person → `he/she/his/her` | plural → `they/their` | org/thing → `it/its`

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The library has _____ own collection of rare books, but also sponsors local exhibits..."*

- (A) they · (B) he · **(C) its ✅** · (D) we

> 💡 `The library` = singular organization (neuter) → `its`. `they` would be plural; `he` = masculine person.

### Q2
> *"Ms. Novak is worried that the floral arrangements...will not arrive in time for _____ reception dinner."*

- **(A) her ✅** · (B) that · (C) are · (D) until

> 💡 Blank before noun `reception dinner` → **possessive adjective**. Refers to Ms. Novak (female, singular) → `her`.

### Q3
> *"The executive committee decided that the task force would not have _____ own decision-making capacity..."*

- **(A) its ✅** · (B) his · (C) her · (D) theirs

> 💡 `the task force` = singular organization → `its`. `theirs` is a possessive pronoun and cannot come before a noun.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `its` for plural noun | `their` | Count the antecedent: plural → `their` |
| `himself` for compound subject | `themselves` | A and B = plural → `-selves` |
| `their` for a company | `its` | Organization = 1 entity = singular = `it/its` |
| `these` for singular noun | `that` | Singular noun → singular demonstrative `that` |
