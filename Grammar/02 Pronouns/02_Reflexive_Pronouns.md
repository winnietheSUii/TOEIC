# 📌 Reflexive Pronouns
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 02 — Pronouns | Part 2 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: When to Use -self / -selves*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Reflexive pronouns end in **`-self`** (singular) or **`-selves`** (plural).
> TOEIC tests two specific uses: **same subject/object** and **emphasis**.

---

## 📋 Reflexive Pronoun Reference Table

| Subject | Reflexive |
|---------|-----------|
| I | **myself** |
| you (singular) | **yourself** |
| he | **himself** |
| she | **herself** |
| it | **itself** |
| we | **ourselves** |
| you (plural) | **yourselves** |
| they | **themselves** |

---

## 📂 Two Uses of Reflexive Pronouns

### ✅ Use 1 — Subject and Object are the SAME person
> The action bounces back to the subject.

> *"Sometimes, Jessica talks to **herself**."* → herself = Jessica (same person)
> *"I have not been able to examine the statement **myself**."*

> Compare:
> *"Sometimes, Jessica talks to **her**."* → her ≠ Jessica (different person)
> → Use regular object pronoun when subject ≠ object.

---

### ✅ Use 2 — Emphasis (optional — can be removed)
> Adds emphasis to the subject. The sentence still works without it.

> *"The manager **himself** guided the group to the factory."* (emphasis on manager)
> *"I didn't have time to read the report **myself**."* (emphasis on I)
> *"She liked the house **itself**, but not the location."* (emphasis on house)

---

## 📋 Fixed Expressions with Reflexive Pronouns

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **by oneself** | alone / on one's own |
| **for oneself** | for one's own benefit |
| **of itself** | naturally / automatically |
| **in itself** | as a quality it possesses |

> *"Karen got the job by **herself**."* = on her own, without help.

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Reflexive vs. Object pronoun:** Does the subject = the object? If YES → reflexive. If NO → regular object pronoun (`him`, `her`, `them`)
- **Trap 2 — Reflexive vs. Possessive:** *"...gave **himself** the award"* = reflexive ✅. *"...gave **his** award to someone"* = possessive adjective
- **Trap 3 — Wrong reflexive form:** Plural subject → plural reflexive. *"Mr. Pak and others tried by **themselves**"* ✅ not `himself`
- **Trap 4 — `by herself` = alone:** Fixed expression meaning "without help" — not interchangeable with `her`

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the subject of the sentence.
> **Step 2:** Does the blank refer back to the **same subject**? → Reflexive pronoun.
> **Step 3:** Is the blank adding **emphasis** to the subject or object? → Reflexive pronoun (optional).
> **Step 4:** Is it a fixed expression (`by oneself`)? → Reflexive pronoun.
> **Step 5:** Otherwise → use regular subject/object/possessive pronoun.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"I have not been able to examine the statement _____, although it has been on my desk for the past two days."*

- (A) me · **(B) myself ✅** · (C) mine · (D) my

> 💡 Subject = `I`. Blank adds **emphasis** to `I` (I myself could not examine it). Reflexive pronoun → `myself`.

### Q2
> *"Chad Reyes's total sales topped everyone else's...so the manager gave the top employee award to _____."*

- (A) him · (B) he · (C) his · **(D) himself ✅**

> 💡 Subject = manager, gives award TO Chad. Subject ≠ object... BUT context: manager gave the award to Chad **himself** (emphatic — Chad personally) → `himself`.

### Q3
> *"Karen got the job by _____, despite rumors that she has friends in high places."*

- (A) her · (B) she · **(C) herself ✅** · (D) hers

> 💡 `by ___` = fixed expression `by herself` = on her own / alone. Reflexive pronoun required.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `him` when subject = object | `himself` | Same person → add `-self` |
| `themselves` for singular subject | `himself/herself` | Match number: singular → `-self`, plural → `-selves` |
| `by her` for "alone" | `by herself` | `by oneself` = fixed expression, always reflexive |
| Reflexive as subject | Never use reflexive as subject | *"Himself went..."* ❌ → *"He went..."* ✅ |
