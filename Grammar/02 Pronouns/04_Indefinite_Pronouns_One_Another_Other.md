# 📌 Indefinite Pronouns & Adjectives (1) — One, Another, Other
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 02 — Pronouns | Part 4 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: one / another / other / the other — distinctions*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> These four words express **unspecified or additional** people/things.
> TOEIC tests your ability to choose the right word based on **how many remain** and whether you've **already specified which one**.

---

## 📋 At a Glance

| Word | Type | Refers to | Noun after it? |
|------|------|-----------|----------------|
| **one** | Pronoun | Unspecified single item (replaces a noun) | No |
| **another** | Pronoun / Adjective | One more (additional / different) | Yes (singular) |
| **other** | Adjective | Different from those mentioned | Yes (plural) |
| **others** | Pronoun | Different ones (stands alone) | No |
| **the other** | Adjective / Pronoun | The remaining one(s) — specific | Yes (singular) or alone |
| **the others** | Pronoun | All remaining ones — specific | No |

---

## 📂 Detailed Rules

### 1️⃣ One — replaces an unspecified singular noun

> A noun must be mentioned **before** `one` can be used.

> *"I sold my old car and bought a new **one**."*
> → `one` replaces `car` (already mentioned)

---

### 2️⃣ Another — one more / an additional one

> - As **adjective**: comes before a **singular noun**
> - As **pronoun**: stands alone
> - **Cannot** come after `the`

> *"One student is from Mexico. **Another** student is from Japan."* (adj + singular noun)
> *"One is from Mexico. **Another** is from Japan."* (pronoun, stands alone)

---

### 3️⃣ Other / Others — different ones

> - **`other`** + **plural noun** (adjective role)
> - **`others`** stands alone (pronoun role)

> *"Other students are from Brazil."* (adj + plural noun)
> *"**Others** are from Brazil."* (pronoun)

---

### 4️⃣ The other / The others — the remaining / specific one(s)

> Use when you know **exactly how many are left**.

| Situation | Use | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| 1 remaining | `the other` + singular / stands alone | *"I have 2 books. One is mine. **The other** is yours."* |
| 2+ remaining | `the other` + plural / `the others` | *"I have 3 books. One is mine. **The other books** are yours."* |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `other` without noun:** `other` must come before a noun. Alone → use `others`
- **Trap 2 — `another` + plural noun:** ❌ impossible. `another` = always singular. For plural → use `other`
- **Trap 3 — `the another`:** ❌ Never. `the` cannot come before `another`
- **Trap 4 — `others` vs `the others`:** `others` = some unspecified different ones. `the others` = all the remaining specific ones

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** How many items are being compared/referred to?
> **Step 2:** Is the noun already **specified/known**? → Use `the other` / `the others`
> **Step 3:** Is it **additional/different** with no specific remainder? → `another` (1) or `others` (many)
> **Step 4:** Is there a noun **directly after** the blank? → adjective form (`another` / `other` / `the other`)
> **Step 5:** Does the blank **stand alone**? → pronoun form (`another` / `others` / `the others`)

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The company has grown so quickly that its owners are planning to set up _____ branch in China and _____ in Japan."*

- (A) each other; one another · **(B) one; another ✅** · (C) other · (D) another

> 💡 First blank: `one branch` (singular, first mentioned). Second blank: `another` (one more, additional). Two separate branches introduced → `one...another`.

### Q2
> *"The company purchased most of its necessities from a single supplier, but _____ were ordered from special sources."*

- (A) other · **(B) others ✅** · (C) the other · (D) both

> 💡 Blank **stands alone** (no noun after) → pronoun form. Not a specific remainder → `others` (some different/additional items). `the other` would imply you know exactly what's left.

### Q3
> *"During the orientation, it was discovered that two of the workers already knew _____."*

- (A) ones · (B) other · (C) another · **(D) others ✅**

> 💡 `knew others` = knew other people (the other workers). Pronoun standing alone, referring to other people in general → `others`.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `other` standing alone | `others` | `other` = adjective → MUST have noun after it |
| `the another` | `another` (no `the`) | `the` + `another` = never valid |
| `another` + plural | `other` + plural | `another` = always 1 |
| `others` vs `the others` | `the others` = the rest (specific) | `the` = we know exactly which ones remain |
