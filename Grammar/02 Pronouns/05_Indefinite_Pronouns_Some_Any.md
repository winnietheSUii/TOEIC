# 📌 Indefinite Pronouns & Adjectives (2) — Some, Any, No, None, Most
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 02 — Pronouns | Part 5 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Sentence Type & Context Matching*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> `some`, `any`, `no`, `none`, and `most` all express **quantity without being specific**.
> Each has rules about **which sentence type** it fits — affirmative, negative, or interrogative.

---

## 📋 Quick Reference Table

| Word | Type | Sentence Type | Noun Type |
|------|------|---------------|-----------|
| **some** | Pronoun / Adjective | Affirmative | Count (plural) + Non-count |
| **any** | Pronoun / Adjective | Negative / Interrogative / Conditional | Count (plural) + Non-count |
| **no** | Adjective only | Any (negative meaning) | Before a noun |
| **none** | Pronoun only | Any | Stands alone (often + `of the`) |
| **most** | Pronoun / Adjective | Any | Count (plural) + Non-count |

---

## 📂 Detailed Rules

### ✅ Some — affirmative & offers
> - Adjective: before noun in **affirmative** sentences
> - Pronoun: stands alone
> - Also used in **offers/requests** (even if question form)

> *"**Some** members of the group didn't show up."* (adjective)
> *"**Some** disapproved of the idea."* (pronoun)
> *"I'm going to buy **some** clothes."* (count noun)
> *"Would you like **some** coffee?"* (offer — affirmative in intent)

---

### ✅ Any — negative, interrogative, conditional
> - Adjective or pronoun
> - Used when the existence of something is **uncertain or denied**

> *"We don't have **any** of the equipment."* (negative)
> *"Have you got **any** luggage?"* (interrogative)
> *"If there are **any** letters for me, send them here."* (conditional)

---

### ✅ No — adjective only (negative meaning, before noun)
> *"There was **no** bus."* → `no` + noun = zero quantity
> Never stands alone. For standalone use → use `none`.

---

### ✅ None — pronoun only (stands alone)
> *"**None** of the shops were open."*
> → `none of the + noun` = a fixed structure

---

### ✅ Most — majority (pronoun or adjective)
> - As **pronoun**: `most of the + noun`
> - As **adjective**: `most + noun` (no `of the`)

> *"**Most** of the people didn't enjoy the party."* (pronoun + `of the`)
> *"**Most** people didn't enjoy the party."* (adjective, no `of the`)

> ⚠️ `most` ≠ `almost`: `almost` is an **adverb** (modifies verbs/adjectives), not a pronoun. `almost` cannot replace `most`.

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `any` in affirmative:** "**Any** members agreed..." ❌ → use `some` in affirmative
- **Trap 2 — `no` standing alone:** "**No** were available" ❌ → `none` stands alone
- **Trap 3 — `most` vs `almost`:** "**almost** of the proposals" ❌ → `most of the proposals` ✅
- **Trap 4 — `the most` vs `most`:** `the most` = superlative (the greatest degree). `most of the` = majority

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** What **sentence type** is it? Affirmative → `some`. Negative/question → `any`.
> **Step 2:** Is the blank **before a noun**? → Adjective form (`some/any/no/most`)
> **Step 3:** Does the blank **stand alone**? → Pronoun form (`some/any/none/most`)
> **Step 4:** Is the blank followed by `of the + noun`? → `most/none/some/any` (pronoun with `of the`)
> **Step 5:** Distinguish `most` (majority) from `almost` (adverb = nearly)

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"_____ of the clerks in the supply room faulted themselves for not accurately recording the inventory."*

- (A) No · (B) One · **(C) Some ✅** · (D) Any

> 💡 Affirmative sentence, positive context (they faulted themselves) → `some`. `No` would mean none faulted themselves — opposite meaning. `Any` = negative/question context.

### Q2
> *"We received the sales figures...but there appears to be _____ of a discrepancy in the third quarter."*

- **(A) something ✅** · (B) everything · (C) anything · (D) nothing

> 💡 Affirmative sentence, a discrepancy **exists** → `something of a discrepancy` = fixed expression meaning "a bit of a problem." `anything` = negative/question; `nothing` = zero/negative meaning.

### Q3
> *"The project approval committee received forty proposals, _____ of which were related to electricity transmission."*

- **(A) most ✅** · (B) the most · (C) almost · (D) any of

> 💡 "_____ of which" = pronoun + relative clause structure → `most of which` = the majority of them. `almost` = adverb (cannot fill this slot). `the most` = superlative.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `any` in affirmative | `some` | Affirmative → `some` / Negative+Question → `any` |
| `no` standing alone | `none` | `no` = before noun; `none` = stands alone |
| `almost of the` | `most of the` | `almost` = adverb; `most` = pronoun/adjective |
| `the most` for majority | `most of` | `the most` = superlative; `most of` = the majority |
