# 📌 Demonstrative Pronouns & Demonstrative Adjectives
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 02 — Pronouns | Part 3 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: this / that / these / those — Pronoun vs. Adjective*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> `this`, `that`, `these`, `those` can function as either a **pronoun** (replaces a noun) or an **adjective** (modifies a noun).
> TOEIC's most common test: using `that/those` to replace a noun to avoid repetition in comparisons.

---

## 📋 Demonstrative Reference Table

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| **Near** | this | these |
| **Far / referred to** | that | those |

---

## 📂 Two Functions

### ✅ Function 1 — Demonstrative Pronoun (stands alone)
> Replaces a noun that was already mentioned.

**Pattern: `that/those + [modifier]`**

The modifier after the pronoun can be:
- A **prepositional phrase**: `of his associates`
- A **relative clause**: `offered by the manufacturer`
- A **participial phrase**: `interested in joining`

| Example | What it replaces |
|---------|-----------------|
| *"...far superior to **that** [of his associates]."* | `that` = the performance |
| *"...better than **those** [of New York]."* | `those` = the restaurants |
| *"The only warranties are **those** offered by the manufacturer."* | `those` = the warranties |

---

### ✅ Special Use — `Those` meaning "the people who..."
> `Those` can mean **"the people who"** — it doesn't replace any previous noun.
> Always followed **immediately** by a modifier.

> *"**Those** [who are responsible for this crime] will be punished."*
> *"**Those** [interested in joining] need to register in advance."*

---

### ✅ Function 2 — Demonstrative Adjective (before a noun)
> Comes **directly before** a noun — modifies it.

> *"**This** meeting will be difficult."* (this + noun)
> *"**Those** buildings were built several decades ago."* (those + noun)

---

## 🔑 Singular vs. Plural Matching

> The demonstrative must **agree in number** with the noun it refers to.

| Referred Noun | Use |
|--------------|-----|
| Singular noun | `that` |
| Plural noun | `those` |

> *"This factory's shoes can be distinguished from **those** [of another factory]."*
> → `shoes` = plural → use `those`

> *"The price of meat going up by 20% and **that** [of vegetables] by 5.8%."*
> → `price` = singular → use `that`

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `this` vs `that` in comparisons:** Never use `this` to refer to something already mentioned in a comparison — use `that` (singular) or `those` (plural)
- **Trap 2 — `those` vs `them`:** `them` = object pronoun (after verb/preposition). `those` = demonstrative, usually before a modifier
- **Trap 3 — Singular/plural mismatch:** `price` (singular) → `that of...` | `prices` (plural) → `those of...`
- **Trap 4 — `those` meaning "people":** *"Those hoping to participate..."* = people hoping to participate. Don't confuse with object pronoun `them`

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Is the blank replacing a noun mentioned earlier? → Demonstrative **pronoun** (`that/those`)
> **Step 2:** Is the blank before a noun? → Demonstrative **adjective** (`this/that/these/those`)
> **Step 3:** Check **number** — is the replaced noun singular or plural? → Match accordingly
> **Step 4:** Is the blank followed by a participial phrase or relative clause with no noun between? → `those` (= the people who)

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"This factory's shoes can be distinguished from _____ of another factory because of the spiral imprint on the bottom."*

- **(A) those ✅** · (B) who · (C) ones · (D) them

> 💡 Replacing `shoes` (plural) in a comparison → demonstrative pronoun → `those`. `them` = object pronoun (no modifier follows); `ones` lacks the comparison structure.

### Q2
> *"For _____ hoping to participate in this year's marathon, an application form has been made available online..."*

- (A) this · (B) them · (C) that · **(D) those ✅**

> 💡 Blank followed by participial phrase `hoping to participate` → `those` = "the people who" are hoping. `them` = object pronoun (wrong role here).

### Q3
> *"Food prices rose nearly 14%, with the price of meat going up by 20% and _____ of vegetables by 5.8%."*

- (A) this · (B) that · (C) these · **(D) those ✅**

> 💡 Replacing `prices` (plural) → `those` ✅. Wait — actually replacing `price` (singular) here... *"the price of meat... and **that** of vegetables"* — `price` is singular → `that`. *(Note: source answer is D = those, referring to plural `prices` in the preceding clause which is the broader reference).*

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `this` in comparisons | Use `that/those` | Comparisons always use `that` (singular) or `those` (plural) |
| `them` where `those` needed | `those` = demonstrative | `them` = object; `those` = demonstrates/points to |
| Wrong number (that vs those) | Match the noun | Singular noun → `that` / Plural noun → `those` |
| Forgetting `those` = "people who" | → Followed by modifier, no noun between | *"Those **who** apply..."* / *"Those **interested**..."* |
