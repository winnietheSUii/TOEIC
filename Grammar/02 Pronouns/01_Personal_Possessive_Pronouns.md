# 📌 Personal Pronouns, Possessive Pronouns & Possessive Adjectives
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 02 — Pronouns | Part 1 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Choosing the Right Pronoun Form*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Pronouns replace nouns to avoid repetition.
> TOEIC tests whether you can pick the **correct form** based on the pronoun's **role in the sentence**.

---

## 📋 The Four Forms — Full Reference Table

| Person | Subject | Object | Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
|--------|---------|--------|---------------------|-------------------|
| I | **I** | **me** | **my** | **mine** |
| We | **we** | **us** | **our** | **ours** |
| You | **you** | **you** | **your** | **yours** |
| He | **he** | **him** | **his** | **his** |
| She | **she** | **her** | **her** | **hers** |
| It | **it** | **it** | **its** | **its** |
| They | **they** | **them** | **their** | **theirs** |

---

## 📂 The Four Roles

### 1️⃣ Subject Pronoun — does the action
> *"**They** were given free tickets to the show."*
> *"**He** will go with us to the park."*
> → Fills the **subject** position of the verb.

### 2️⃣ Object Pronoun — receives the action / after a preposition
> *"The advisor helped **them** understand the legal situation."* (object of verb)
> *"Mr. Wilson works with **me**."* (object of preposition)
> → Fills the **object** position — after verb or preposition.

### 3️⃣ Possessive Adjective — shows ownership, comes **before a noun**
> *"Many people make decisions based on **their** emotions."*
> → Always followed by a noun. **Cannot** stand alone.

### 4️⃣ Possessive Pronoun — shows ownership, stands **alone** (no noun after)
> *"My book is about plants, but **hers** is about herbs."*
> → Replaces `possessive adjective + noun`. **Never** before a noun.

---

## 🔑 Key Distinction: Possessive Adjective vs. Possessive Pronoun

| | Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
|---|---|---|
| Follows a noun? | ✅ Yes — always before noun | ❌ No — stands alone |
| Example | `their emotions` | `hers` (= her book) |
| Can it replace a noun? | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |

> ✅ *"**Their** proposal was accepted."* (adjective before noun)
> ✅ *"The proposal was **theirs**."* (pronoun stands alone)
> ❌ *"The **theirs** proposal was accepted."* (pronoun before noun = wrong)

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Object vs. Subject:** After a comma with a new subject → use **subject pronoun**. *"When they arrived, **they** were picked up."* ✅ not `them`
- **Trap 2 — Possessive adjective vs. pronoun:** Blank before a noun → **possessive adjective** (`his`, `their`). Blank standing alone → **possessive pronoun** (`his`, `theirs`)
- **Trap 3 — Reflexive in object slot:** *"...delivering **his** proposal"* → needs possessive adjective, not `himself`
- **Trap 4 — `its` vs `it's`:** `its` = possessive adjective (no apostrophe). `it's` = it is (contraction — never used in TOEIC answers)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the blank — where is it in the sentence?
> **Step 2:** Is there a **noun directly after** the blank? → Possessive **adjective**
> **Step 3:** Does the blank **stand alone** (no noun after)? → Possessive **pronoun**
> **Step 4:** Is it the **subject** of a verb? → Subject pronoun (`I/we/he/she/they`)
> **Step 5:** Is it after a **verb or preposition**? → Object pronoun (`me/us/him/her/them`)

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"When the trade negotiators arrived at the airport, _____ were picked up and brought to their hotel."*

- (A) them · (B) **they ✅** · (C) their · (D) theirs

> 💡 Blank is the **subject** of `were picked up` → subject pronoun → `they`.

### Q2
> *"After delivering _____ proposal, Mr. Douglas sat with committee advisors..."*

- (A) their · (B) himself · **(C) his ✅** · (D) him

> 💡 Blank before noun `proposal` → **possessive adjective**. Subject = Mr. Douglas (male, singular) → `his`.

### Q3
> *"...you may contact _____ at any of the following telephone numbers..."*

- (A) we · **(B) us ✅** · (C) our · (D) ours

> 💡 After verb `contact` → **object pronoun** → `us`.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `them` as subject | `they` | Subject pronoun = does the action |
| `theirs proposal` | `their proposal` | Possessive pronoun never before noun |
| `himself` in possessive slot | `his` | `-self` = reflexive (same subject/object), not possessive |
| `its` confusion | No apostrophe for possession | `it's` = it is; `its` = belonging to it |
