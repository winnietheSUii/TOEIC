# 📌 Subject-Verb Agreement with Relative Clauses
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 09 — Subject-Verb Agreement | Part 4 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Gerund Subjects · Collective Nouns · Error Identification Drill*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> This note consolidates all SVA rules and adds two advanced special cases:
> **gerund phrases as subjects** (always singular) and **collective nouns** (singular in meaning).
> Use this as your pre-exam rapid-fire review.

---

## 📋 All SVA Rules — Master Reference

| Subject Type | Verb | Example |
|-------------|------|---------|
| `every/each + noun` | Singular | *"Every employee **needs**..."* |
| `one/each of the + plural noun` | Singular | *"Each of the trainees **has**..."* |
| `somebody/everyone/nobody` | Singular | *"Nobody **was** harmed."* |
| `many/several/both + plural noun` | Plural | *"Many employees **have**..."* |
| `a number of / a variety of + plural noun` | Plural | *"A variety of methods **are**..."* |
| `A and B` / `Both A and B` | Plural | *"The CEO and the CFO **are**..."* |
| `A or B` | Match nearest (B) | *"A bus or two vans **are**..."* |
| `Either A or B` / `Neither A nor B` | Match B | *"Either he or they **are**..."* |
| `A, along with / together with B,` | Match A only | *"The manager, along with staff, **is**..."* |
| `half/all/most of the + singular noun` | Singular | *"Half of the profit **goes**..."* |
| `half/all/most of the + plural noun` | Plural | *"Half of the employees **commute**..."* |
| Relative clause → antecedent | Match antecedent | *"Offices that **are** leased..."* |
| `the number of + plural noun` | Singular | *"The number of users **requires**..."* |
| **Gerund phrase** | Singular | *"Reducing waste **is** practical."* |
| **Collective noun** | Singular | *"The news **warns** of..."* |

---

## 📂 Special Case 1 — Gerund Phrase as Subject = Singular

> A **gerund phrase** acting as the subject is always treated as a **singular** unit.

> ✅ *"**Reducing waste** through increased recycling **is** both practical and necessary."*
> ❌ *"Reducing waste...are..."* — `reducing waste` = one concept = singular

> Other examples:
> - *"**Operating the machinery is** difficult."*
> - *"**Meeting the deadline requires** planning."*

---

## 📂 Special Case 2 — Collective Nouns = Singular

> Collective nouns refer to a **group** but are treated as a **single entity** in English.
> → Always use a **singular verb**.

| Collective Noun | Example |
|----------------|---------|
| `news` | *"The **news warns** that the airline industry is on the verge of collapse."* |
| `information` | *"Accurate **information is** needed."* |
| `committee` | *"The **committee has** reached a decision."* |
| `staff` | *"The **staff is** ready."* |
| `government` | *"The **government is** pushing for reform."* |
| `company` | *"The **company has** released its report."* |
| `team` | *"The **team was** selected."* |
| `management` | *"Upper **management is** reviewing the policy."* |

> ⚠️ `news` is a common trap — it ends in `-s` but is **always singular**.

## 🔍 Error Identification Drill — All Patterns

| Sentence | Error | Correction | Rule |
|----------|-------|-----------|------|
| *"Plans to open an office in Asia **was** delayed."* | `was` | `were` | `Plans` = plural |
| *"Employees at this firm that **is** currently salaried..."* | `is` | `are` | Antecedent `Employees` = plural |
| *"**Much** fear that small business taxes will rise."* | `Much` | `Many` | `fear` = countable here → `many` |
| *"All computers in the office **was** damaged."* | `was` | `were` | `All computers` = plural |
| *"A car and a truck **is** on sale."* | `is` | `are` | `and` → compound → plural |
| *"Half of the stocks **pays** annual dividends."* | `pays` | `pay` | `stocks` after `of the` = plural |
| *"The news **warn** that the airline is in trouble."* | `warn` | `warns` | `news` = collective → singular |
| *"**Uniform** with company emblems are required."* | `Uniform` | `Uniforms` | Subject should be plural to match `are` |
| *"Reducing waste **are** practical."* | `are` | `is` | Gerund phrase = singular |
| *"The supervisor, together with investigators, **are** going to confront..."* | `are` | `is` | `together with` = modifier; main subject = `supervisor` (singular) |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `news` = singular:** Ends in `-s` but never plural. *"The news **is**..."* ✅
- **Trap 2 — Gerund = singular:** `"Reducing waste **is**..."` not `are`
- **Trap 3 — `together with` doesn't add:** Only `and` creates a compound subject
- **Trap 4 — `Much` vs `Many` before countable noun:** countable → `many` · non-count → `much`

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy — 4-Step Checklist

> **Step 1:** Identify the SUBJECT. Strip away all modifiers, appositives, and prepositional phrases.
> **Step 2:** Is the subject joined by a **conjunction**?
> - `and` → plural
> - `or/nor / either-or / neither-nor` → match subject B
> - `along with / together with` → ignore, match subject A
> **Step 3:** Is the subject a **special form**?
> - Gerund phrase → singular
> - Collective noun → singular
> - `every/each/one + noun` → singular
> - `a variety/number of` → plural
> **Step 4:** Is there a **relative clause** involved? → Trace antecedent, ignore distractors between.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Plans to open an office in Asia _____ delayed for several months."*

- (A) was · **(B) were ✅** · (C) has been · (D) will be

> 💡 `Plans` = plural → `were`. Modifier `to open an office in Asia` is not the subject.

### Q2
> *"The supervisor, together with investigators, _____ going to confront the accountant about fraud."*

- **(A) is ✅** · (B) are · (C) have · (D) has

> 💡 `together with investigators` = modifier → ignored. Main subject = `supervisor` (singular) → `is`.

### Q3
> *"Half of the stocks listed on the exchange _____ annual dividends."*

- (A) pays · **(B) pay ✅** · (C) paying · (D) have paid

> 💡 `Half of the + stocks` → noun after `of the` = `stocks` (plural) → plural verb → `pay`.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Always Singular | Always Plural |
|----------------|--------------|
| `every / each / one` + noun | `many / several / few / both` + noun |
| `everyone / nobody / someone` | `a number of / a variety of` + noun |
| Gerund phrase as subject | `A and B` compound |
| Collective nouns (news/staff/team) | `all / half / most of the` + **plural** noun |
| `the number of` + plural noun | `a number of` + plural noun |

> ✏️ **Speed Test — Cover the right column:**
> - `The news ___` → `warns`
> - `Half of the staff ___` → `is`
> - `Half of the employees ___` → `are`
> - `A variety of issues ___` → `arise`
> - `The committee ___` → `has`
> - `Reducing costs ___` → `is`
