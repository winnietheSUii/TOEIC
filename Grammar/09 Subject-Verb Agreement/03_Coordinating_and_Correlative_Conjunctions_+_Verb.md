# 📌 Coordinating and Correlative Conjunctions + Verb
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 09 — Subject-Verb Agreement | Part 3 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Antecedent → Relative Clause Verb Agreement*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Inside a **relative clause** (`who / which / that`), the verb must agree with its **antecedent** — the noun that the relative pronoun refers back to.
> The #1 trap: a modifier between the antecedent and the relative clause makes you match the verb to the **wrong noun**.

---

## 📐 The Pattern

```
[Antecedent] + [modifier phrase] + [relative pronoun] + [verb in relative clause]
      ↑                                                         ↑
This determines the verb                              Must match the antecedent
```

---

## 📋 Basic Rule

| Antecedent | Relative Clause Verb |
|------------|---------------------|
| Singular | Singular |
| Plural | Plural |

> *"We have hired an associate **who has** experience in international sales."*
> → `associate` = singular → `has` ✅

> *"Receptionists **who don't have** database experience are useless in today's job market."*
> → `Receptionists` = plural → `don't have` ✅

---

## ⚠️ The Distractor Trap — Modifier Between Antecedent and Relative Clause

> TOEIC inserts a modifier phrase between the antecedent and the relative clause to distract you.
> Always trace back to the **true antecedent** — ignore everything in between.

### Example 1
> *"Offices **in this building** that **are** currently leased on a monthly basis will be subject to additional charges."*
> → Antecedent = `Offices` (plural) — not `building` (singular, in the modifier phrase)
> → `are` ✅

### Example 2
> *"The virus **in the computers**, which **was** removed by a computer technician."*
> → Antecedent = `virus` (singular) — not `computers` (plural, in the modifier phrase)
> → `was` ✅

---

## 📋 Special Case — `the number of` vs. `a number of`

> These two expressions look almost identical but behave oppositely.

| Expression | Verb | Logic |
|------------|------|-------|
| **`the number of` + plural noun** | **Singular** | `the number` = the count itself = singular |
| **`a number of` + plural noun** | **Plural** | `a number of` = many = plural |

> *"**The number** of users who **requires** support..."*
> → Antecedent of `who` = `number` (singular) → `requires` ✅

> *"**A number of** users **require** support."*
> → `a number of` = plural → `require` ✅

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Distractor noun closer to verb:** *"The virus **in the computers**, which **were**..."* ❌ → `virus` = singular → `was` ✅
- **Trap 2 — `the number of` treated as plural:** `The number of users **require**` ❌ → `requires` ✅ (`the number` = singular)
- **Trap 3 — `a number of` treated as singular:** `A number of complaints **has**` ❌ → `have` ✅
- **Trap 4 — Plural modifier before singular antecedent:** Find the HEAD noun of the subject, not the modifier

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the relative pronoun (`who / which / that`).
> **Step 2:** Trace back to its **antecedent** — the noun it refers to. Skip over any modifier phrases.
> **Step 3:** Is the antecedent singular or plural?
> **Step 4:** Match the relative clause verb to the antecedent's number.
> **Step 5:** For `the/a number of` → ask: is it `the number` (= singular) or `a number of` (= many = plural)?

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Files that _____ in the main facility are being removed in an effort to computerize all records."*

- **(A) were stored ✅** · (B) have stored · (C) was stored · (D) stores

> 💡 Antecedent = `Files` (plural) → relative clause verb must be plural → `were stored`. `was stored` = singular ❌.

### Q2
> *"Each year, we send representatives to job fairs that _____ at college campuses throughout the country."*

- (A) held · **(B) are held ✅** · (C) are holding · (D) was held

> 💡 Antecedent = `job fairs` (plural) → plural verb → `are held` (passive, plural). `was held` = singular ❌.

### Q3
> *"The number of users who _____ support has increased since the company released the latest version."*

- (A) required · **(B) requires ✅** · (C) is required · (D) are required

> 💡 `The number of users who _____` → antecedent of `who` = `number` (singular) → `requires` ✅. `are required` = plural ❌; `required` = past tense ❌.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| Matching verb to distractor noun | Identify antecedent | *"Offices **in this building** that **are**..."* — skip `building` |
| `the number of` → plural | Singular | `the **number**` = the quantity itself = singular entity |
| `a number of` → singular | Plural | `a number of` = "many" = plural in meaning |
| Forgetting relative pronoun traces back | Find head noun | Relative clause verb = mirror of antecedent |
