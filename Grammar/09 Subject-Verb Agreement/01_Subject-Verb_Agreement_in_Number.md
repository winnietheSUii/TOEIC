# 📌 Subject-Verb Agreement in Number
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 09 — Subject-Verb Agreement | Part 1 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Singular & Plural Quantity Expressions + "Of The" Rule*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Subject and verb must **always match in number**.
> The trickiest part is not simple sentences — it's when **quantity expressions** sit between the subject and verb and disguise whether the subject is singular or plural.

---

## 📋 Part 1 — Fixed Singular & Plural Quantity Expressions

### 🔹 Always Singular → Singular Verb

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| `one + singular noun` | *"**One** of the trainees **has** scored perfect."* |
| `each + singular noun` | *"**Each employee** **needs** to register."* |
| `every + singular noun` | *"**Every person** at this meeting **looks** exhausted."* |
| `somebody / someone / something` | *"**Somebody** **is** waiting outside."* |
| `anybody / anyone / anything` | *"**Anyone** who applies **is** welcome."* |
| `everybody / everyone / everything` | *"**Everyone** **was** informed."* |
| `nobody / no one / nothing` | *"**Nobody** **was** harmed."* |

### 🔹 Always Plural → Plural Verb

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| `many + plural noun` | *"**Many schools** **have** football teams."* |
| `several + plural noun` | *"**Several guests** **have** arrived."* |
| `few + plural noun` | *"**Few employees** **were** present."* |
| `both + plural noun` | *"**Both options** **are** available."* |
| `one/each of the + plural noun` | *"**Each of the trainees** **has** scored..."* *(verb = singular — matches `each`, not noun)* |
| `a number of + plural noun` | *"**A number of** complaints **have** been received."* |
| `a couple/variety of + plural noun` | *"**A variety of** methods **are** used."* |

> ⚠️ **Key note on `one/each of the + plural noun`:** The verb matches `one` or `each` — **singular** — even though the noun after `of the` is plural.
> *"**Each of the employees** **has** a badge."* ✅ (not `have`)

---

## 📐 Part 2 — "Of The" Rule: Verb Matches the Noun AFTER `of the`

> When the subject contains a **partitive expression** (a fraction, percentage, or general quantity), the verb agrees with the **noun after `of the`**, not the quantity word.

**Applies to:** `all / most / any / some / half / a lot / lots / part / the rest / the bulk / [X] percent / a fraction`

| Noun after `of the` | Verb | Example |
|--------------------|------|---------|
| Singular | Singular | *"**Half of the annual profit goes** to taxes."* |
| Plural | Plural | *"**Half of the employees commute** by bus."* |
| Singular | Singular | *"**All of the equipment is** functioning."* |
| Plural | Plural | *"**All of the suggestions are** taken into account."* |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `every` = singular:** `every + noun` always → singular verb. *"Every employee **needs**..."* ✅
- **Trap 2 — `a variety of` = plural:** Despite `a`, the head of the phrase is `variety of [plural]` → plural verb
- **Trap 3 — `each of the + plural noun`:** `each` controls → singular verb. *"Each of the employees **has**..."* ✅
- **Trap 4 — `of the` partitive:** Ignore the quantity word. Look at noun AFTER `of the` → that's your subject for agreement

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the subject — is there a quantity expression?
> **Step 2:** Check which category it falls into:
> - Fixed singular (`every/each/one/someone`) → singular verb
> - Fixed plural (`many/several/both/a variety of`) → plural verb
> - Partitive (`half of the / all of the / most of the`) → look at noun AFTER `of the`
> **Step 3:** Ignore any modifying phrases between subject and verb.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"A variety of colors _____ to be used in the painting."*

- (A) is going · **(B) are going ✅** · (C) has · (D) have

> 💡 `A variety of` = always plural → `are going`.

### Q2
> *"Every employee _____ to consider what his or her prospects are for career advancement."*

- (A) need · **(B) needs ✅** · (C) have · (D) has

> 💡 `Every employee` = singular → singular verb → `needs`.

### Q3
> *"All of the consultants' suggestions _____ into consideration by the CEO."*

- (A) is taken · **(B) are taken ✅** · (C) will take · (D) takes

> 💡 `All of the + suggestions` → noun after `of the` = `suggestions` (plural) → `are taken`.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Expression | Verb | Trap |
|------------|------|------|
| `every + noun` | Singular | Never plural — even with `every employee**s**` (invalid) |
| `a variety / number of` | Plural | `a` is misleading — look at `variety/number of [plural]` |
| `each of the + plural noun` | Singular | `each` = singular; ignore the plural noun after `of the` |
| `half of the + singular noun` | Singular | Noun after `of the` controls |
| `half of the + plural noun` | Plural | Noun after `of the` controls |
| `all of the + singular noun` | Singular | e.g. `all of the equipment IS` |
| `all of the + plural noun` | Plural | e.g. `all of the suggestions ARE` |
