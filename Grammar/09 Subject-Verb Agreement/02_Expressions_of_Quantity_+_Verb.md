# 📌 Expressions of Quantity + Verb
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 09 — Subject-Verb Agreement | Part 2 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: And / Or / Correlative Conjunctions / Pseudo-Connectors*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> When two subjects are joined by a conjunction, the verb number changes depending on **which conjunction is used**.
> Some linking phrases look like they expand the subject — but they don't. You must know which is which.

---

## 📂 Rule 1 — `and` → Always Plural

> When two subjects are joined by `and`, they form a **compound subject** → always **plural verb**.

> *"**The president and the director are** working on the plan."* ✅
> *"**Both the flower bed and the garden need** to be watered."* ✅

| Structure | Verb |
|-----------|------|
| `A and B` | Plural |
| `Both A and B` | Plural |

---

## 📂 Rule 2 — `or` → Proximity Rule (Match the Nearest Subject)

> When two subjects are joined by `or`, the verb matches the **subject closest to the verb**.

> *"**Two vans** or **a bus is** needed to take those children somewhere."*
> → `a bus` is closer to the verb → singular → `is` ✅

> *"**A bus** or **two vans are** needed."*
> → `two vans` is closer → plural → `are` ✅

---

## 📂 Rule 3 — Correlative Conjunctions → Match Subject B (the second one)

> The verb agrees with the **second subject** (B) — the one directly before the verb.

| Conjunction | Rule | Example |
|-------------|------|---------|
| `Either A or B` | Match B | *"Either the manager or the **assistants are** responsible."* |
| `Neither A nor B` | Match B | *"Neither the CEO nor the **managers were** informed."* |
| `Not A but B` | Match B | *"Not the staff but the **manager is** in charge."* |
| `Not only A but (also) B` | Match B | *"Not only students but also the **teacher was** surprised."* |

> 💡 **Memory Trick:** With `or/nor`, always look at the **last subject before the verb** — that's the one that matters.

---

## 📂 Rule 4 — Pseudo-Connectors → Verb Matches MAIN Subject (Ignore Them)

> These phrases **look like** they add to the subject, but they don't change the verb number.
> They're **modifiers**, not conjunctions.

| Pseudo-connector | Effect on verb |
|-----------------|---------------|
| `along with` | None |
| `together with` | None |
| `in addition to` | None |
| `as well as` | None |
| `accompanied by` | None |

> *"**Mr. Clarion**, along with his partners, **thinks** the new venture will work."*
> → `Mr. Clarion` = singular → `thinks` ✅ (`along with his partners` = invisible for agreement)

> *"**The federal government**, along with leading industrial companies, **is pushing** to create jobs."*
> → `government` = singular → `is pushing` ✅

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `along with` looks like `and`:** `A, along with B, is...` ❌ → NOT `are`. `along with` ≠ `and`
- **Trap 2 — `or` proximity ignored:** Always match the **last subject** before the verb
- **Trap 3 — `both A and B` = plural:** Even if both A and B are singular → compound = plural
- **Trap 4 — `neither...nor` reversed:** Verb matches B (the second one): `Neither the CEO nor the **managers were**...` → `were` matches `managers` ✅

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** How are the subjects connected?
> - `and` / `Both...and` → **plural** (always)
> - `or` / `nor` → match the **nearest subject**
> - `Either...or` / `Neither...nor` / `Not...but` → match **subject B**
> - `along with` / `together with` / `as well as` → **ignore** these; match main subject only
> **Step 2:** Identify the main subject (ignore anything in commas or after pseudo-connectors).
> **Step 3:** Choose singular or plural verb accordingly.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Mr. Burns and the secretary of our Arlington branch _____ to the weekend gathering."*

- (A) is coming · **(B) are coming ✅** · (C) comes · (D) has come

> 💡 `Mr. Burns **and** the secretary` = compound subject (`and`) → **plural** → `are coming`.

### Q2
> *"The chef's assistant and a special menu _____ for this evening's wine tasting event."*

- (A) am preparing · (B) prepares · **(C) are preparing ✅** · (D) as prepared

> 💡 Two subjects joined by `and` → **plural** → `are preparing`.

### Q3
> *"The federal government, along with the cooperation of leading industrial companies, _____ to create more jobs for skilled workers."*

- **(A) is pushing ✅** · (B) are pushing · (C) pushed · (D) have been pushing

> 💡 Main subject = `The federal government` (singular). `along with...` = modifier → ignored for agreement → singular → `is pushing`.

|-----------|------|------|
| `A and B` | Compound → always plural | Plural |
| `Both A and B` | Same as `and` | Plural |
| `A or B` | Proximity → match nearest | Depends on B |
| `Either A or B` | Match B | Depends on B |
| `Neither A nor B` | Match B | Depends on B |
| `Not A but B` | Match B | Depends on B |
| `A, along with B,` | Ignore B → match A | Depends on A |
| `A, together with B,` | Ignore B → match A | Depends on A |
| `A, as well as B,` | Ignore B → match A | Depends on A |
