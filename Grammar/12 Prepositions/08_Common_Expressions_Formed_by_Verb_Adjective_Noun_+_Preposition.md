# 08_Common_Expressions_Formed_by_Verb_Adjective_Noun_+_Preposition

## 📌 Overview
This is the **single most important preposition unit for TOEIC Part 5.**

Many TOEIC questions test whether you know the correct preposition that follows a specific verb, adjective, or noun. There is **no rule** — you must **memorize** these collocations.

This unit covers:
1. **Verb + Preposition** — comply with, depend on, contribute to
2. **Adjective + Preposition** — responsible for, similar to, consistent with
3. **Noun + Preposition** — access to, increase in, solution to
4. **Fixed Multi-Preposition Expressions** — in charge of, on behalf of, by means of

## 🧠 Core Rules

### Rule 1 — Verb + Preposition is fixed — NO logic, just memorize
```
❌ "comply to" → ✅ "comply WITH"
❌ "depend in" → ✅ "depend ON"
❌ "consist in" → ✅ "consist OF"
❌ "contribute for" → ✅ "contribute TO"
```

### Rule 2 — Some verbs change meaning with different prepositions
```
agree WITH someone    (share their opinion)
agree ON something    (reach consensus about a topic)
agree TO something    (accept a plan/proposal)

apply FOR a position  (submit an application)
apply TO a situation  (be relevant to)

result IN something   (cause something)
result FROM something (be caused by something)
```

### Rule 3 — "increase/decrease/rise/drop" always → "in"
```
✅ "an increase IN sales"
✅ "a decrease IN revenue"
✅ "a drop IN temperature"
❌ "an increase of sales" (WRONG)
```

### Rule 4 — "effect/impact/influence" always → "on"
```
✅ "an effect ON the environment"
✅ "an impact ON revenue"
✅ "influence ON the decision"
❌ "an effect to" (WRONG)
```

### Rule 5 — "solution/access/exposure" always → "to"
```
✅ "a solution TO the problem"
✅ "access TO the database"
✅ "exposure TO new markets"
```

## ⚠️ TOEIC Traps

| Trap | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| comply | comply to | comply **with** |
| responsible | responsible of | responsible **for** |
| increase | increase of sales | increase **in** sales |
| impact | impact to revenue | impact **on** revenue |
| familiar | familiar to the system | familiar **with** the system |
| solution | solution of the problem | solution **to** the problem |
| consist | consist in | consist **of** |
| agree | agree with the plan | agree **to** the plan / agree **on** the plan |
| subject | subject of approval | subject **to** approval |

## 🎯 Exam Thinking Strategy
1. **See a verb/adj/noun before the blank?** → This is a **collocation question** → recall the fixed preposition
2. **Don't try to guess by meaning** → "comply with" has no logical reason — just memorize it
3. **Focus on high-frequency pairs:** comply **with**, responsible **for**, increase **in**, impact **on**, access **to**, solution **to**
4. **"in ___ of" pattern?** → check: charge / place / honor / violation / observance / favor / accordance
5. **"on ___ of" pattern?** → behalf (on behalf of)

## ✏️ Practice Questions

**Q1.** Management has decided to transfer certain responsibilities formerly held ___ the administrative division ___ the accounting department.
- (A) at, by
- (B) **by, to** ✅
- (C) to, of
- (D) on, at

> **Why (B):** Two collocations here: "held **by** the administrative division" (passive voice — agent introduced by "by") and "transfer ___ **to** the accounting department" (transfer A to B = move A to destination B).
> (A) "held at" = held at a location, not by an agent. (C) "held to" doesn't work for possession. (D) "held on" doesn't work here.

---

**Q2.** Education for Children, Inc. has always been an advocate ___ effective early education through home- and school-based programs.
- (A) to
- (B) by
- (C) **for** ✅
- (D) over

> **Why (C):** "an advocate **for**" = a supporter of. The noun "advocate" is always followed by **for** (or occasionally "of") when stating what is being supported.
> (A) "advocate to" is not standard. (B) "advocate by" is not standard. (D) "advocate over" is not standard.

---

**Q3.** Ms. Turner asked us to use the original design ___ the product display ___ the one submitted by the team.
- (A) **for, instead of** ✅
- (B) instead of, at
- (C) at, instead of
- (D) of, instead of

> **Why (A):** "design **for** the product display" = the design intended for/purposed for the display (purpose). "**Instead of** the one submitted" = replacing the team's submission.
> (B) "instead of the product display" reverses the meaning. (C) "at the product display" = location, not purpose. (D) "design of" = possession, not purpose.
