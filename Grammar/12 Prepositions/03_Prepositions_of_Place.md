# 03_Prepositions_of_Place

## 📌 Overview
Prepositions of place in TOEIC appear in two ways:
1. **Literal place** — physical position of objects or people
2. **Extended meaning** — state, condition, relationship (non-physical)

The extended meanings are the HIGH-YIELD traps in Part 5 — especially **"under + noun"** fixed expressions.

---

## 📊 Reference Table — BASIC PLACE

| Preposition | Literal Meaning | Example |
|-------------|----------------|---------|
| **above / over** | higher than | above the sink, over his head |
| **below / under** | lower than | below the table, under the bridge |
| **beside / next to** | at the side of | beside the window |
| **behind** | at the back of | behind the building |
| **between** | in the middle of two | between A and B |
| **among** | in the middle of many | among the crowd |
| **near / close to** | not far from | near the exit |
| **within** | inside the boundaries of | within the building |
| **around** | surrounding / in the general area | around the corner |

---

## 📊 Reference Table — EXTENDED MEANINGS (TOEIC HIGH-YIELD)

| Preposition | Extended Meaning | Fixed Expressions |
|-------------|----------------|------------------|
| **over** | in authority / control of | supervision over, authority over |
| **under** | in a state of / being processed | under review, under investigation, under development, under consideration, under new management, under contract, under construction, under pressure, under warranty |
| **between** | connection / difference | relationship between, difference between, agreement between |
| **within** | inside scope / range / org | within the organization, within budget, within reach, within the scope of |
| **around** | throughout / general area | around the world, around the clock, around the corner |
| **below** | less than / lower than a level | below average, below expectations, below normal |
| **above** | more than / higher than a level | above average, above the limit |
| **among** | within a group (3+) | among employees, among the options |

---

## 🧠 Core Rules

### Rule 1 — between vs among
```
BETWEEN = 2 things / people
AMONG   = 3 or more / a group

✅ "between A and B"
✅ "among the employees"
❌ "among the two candidates" (only 2 → use between)
```

### Rule 2 — above vs over / below vs under
```
ABOVE / BELOW = static position (no contact)
OVER  / UNDER = can imply coverage or movement

✅ "The temperature is above 30°C." (level comparison)
✅ "She put a blanket over him." (coverage/movement)
✅ "The score fell below average." (level comparison)
✅ "The box is under the desk." (position)
```

### Rule 3 — "under + noun" fixed expressions (TOEIC TRAP)
```
under construction    = being built
under review          = being examined
under investigation   = being investigated
under consideration   = being thought about
under development     = being developed
under new management  = with a new manager
under contract        = covered by a contract
under pressure        = experiencing pressure
under warranty        = covered by a guarantee
```
> ⚠️ These are ALL fixed — always "under", never "in" or "on"

---

## ⚠️ TOEIC Traps

| Trap | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| between/among | between many employees | **among** many employees |
| "under" fixed | in construction | **under** construction |
| "under" fixed | in review | **under** review |
| level comparison | over average | **above** average / **below** average |
| "within" scope | inside the organization | **within** the organization |

---

## 🎯 Exam Thinking Strategy
1. **Is it a "state" or "process"?** → likely **under** + noun (check fixed expression list)
2. **Is it a level comparison?** → above/below average, below normal
3. **2 items or many?** → between (2) / among (3+)
4. **Inside an org or boundary?** → **within** (not "in" for formal TOEIC context)
5. **"around the world / around the clock"** → memorize as fixed expressions

## ✏️ Practice Questions

**Q1.** A recent study mentions that prescription drug use has steadily increased ___ people aged 45 years and above over the past two decades.
- (A) **among** ✅
- (B) between
- (C) with
- (D) around

> **Why (A):** "people aged 45 and above" = a large group (many people), not just two → **among** is correct for groups of 3+.
> (B) "between" is for exactly two items. (C) "with" doesn't indicate distribution within a group. (D) "around" means approximately, not within a group.

---

**Q2.** Last summer's heavy precipitation caused sales of beachwear to fall 20 percent ___ normal.
- (A) after
- (B) **below** ✅
- (C) behind
- (D) beside

> **Why (B):** "below normal" = lower than the standard level — a level comparison.
> (A) "after normal" is not a level expression. (C) "behind" means at the back of or delayed, not a level. (D) "beside" means next to, not a comparison.

---

**Q3.** The annex to the office building on Lester Avenue will be ___ construction until April.
- (A) in
- (B) **under** ✅
- (C) within
- (D) around

> **Why (B):** "**under construction**" = a fixed expression meaning the building is being built.
> (A) "in construction" is not the standard fixed expression. (C) "within construction" is not an expression. (D) "around construction" does not exist in this context.
