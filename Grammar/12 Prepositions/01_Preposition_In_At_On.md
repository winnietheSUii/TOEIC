# 01_Preposition_In_At_On

## 📌 Overview
**In / At / On** are the three most common prepositions in TOEIC.
They appear in **time**, **place**, and **fixed expression** contexts.
Choosing the wrong one is a top Part 5 trap — they are almost always the 4 answer choices together.

---

## 📊 Reference Table — TIME

| Preposition | Used With | Examples |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| **IN** | Months, years, seasons, centuries, parts of day | in August, in 2008, in winter, in the morning |
| **AT** | Specific clock times, points of time | at 7:00, at noon, at night, at midnight, at the beginning/end |
| **ON** | Days of week, specific dates, holidays | on Friday, on August 15, on Christmas Day |

> ⚡ **Memory trick:** IN = big period → AT = exact point → ON = named day/date

---

## 📊 Reference Table — PLACE

| Preposition | Used With | Examples |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| **IN** | Enclosed spaces, cities, countries, regions | in the room, in the city, in New York |
| **AT** | Specific locations, addresses, intersections | at the bus stop, at Franklin Street, at the office |
| **ON** | Surfaces, floors, roads, rivers, walls | on the table, on the first floor, on the wall |

---

## 📊 Reference Table — FIXED EXPRESSIONS (High TOEIC Frequency)

| Expression | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **in place** | ready / set up correctly |
| **on time** | punctually |
| **at least** | minimum |
| **in time** | early enough / before deadline |
| **at the rate of** | at a speed/frequency of |
| **at your earliest convenience** | as soon as you can (formal) |
| **on a regular basis** | regularly |
| **on the waiting list** | queued |
| **on the recommendation of** | following advice of |
| **at a good pace** | at a reasonable speed |
| **in the foreseeable future** | soon |
| **in the coming year** | next year |

---

## 🧠 Core Rules

### Rule 1 — Time Hierarchy
```
Biggest period → IN
Exact point    → AT
Named day/date → ON
```

### Rule 2 — Place Hierarchy
```
Inside enclosed space   → IN
Specific point/address  → AT
Surface or named road   → ON
```

### Rule 3 — The "in place" trap
> "All machines are **in place**" ≠ "at place" or "on place"
> **in place** = fixed expression meaning "ready / positioned"

---

## ⚠️ TOEIC Traps

| Trap | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| Specific time | on 7:00 | **at** 7:00 |
| Part of day | at the morning | **in** the morning |
| Exception: night | in night | **at** night |
| Exception: weekend | in the weekend | **at** the weekend (UK) / **on** the weekend (US) |
| Fixed: "on time" | in time (= early enough) | **on** time (= punctual) |
| Fixed: "in place" | at place | **in** place |

---

## 🎯 Exam Thinking Strategy
1. **Is it a time expression?** → Check: period (IN) / point (AT) / day-date (ON)
2. **Is it a place expression?** → Check: enclosed (IN) / specific point (AT) / surface (ON)
3. **Is it a fixed expression?** → Memorize the common ones above
4. If you see all four options are **in / at / on / for** → it's a fixed expression question → recall the collocation

## ✏️ Practice Questions

**Q1.** The interchange with the real estate consultant is expected to take place tomorrow afternoon ___ 4:00.
- (A) on
- (B) **at** ✅
- (C) to
- (D) for

> **Why (B):** "4:00" is a specific clock time → **at** specific times.
> (A) "on" is for days/dates, not clock times. (C) "to" shows direction. (D) "for" shows duration.

---

**Q2.** If our new product becomes a hot seller in Springfield, we are going to test-market it ___ 25 major cities across the US.
- (A) on
- (B) **in** ✅
- (C) against
- (D) to

> **Why (B):** "cities" = enclosed geographic areas → **in** cities.
> (A) "on" is for surfaces/days. (C) "against" means opposition. (D) "to" shows direction only.

---

**Q3.** All of the drilling machines are ___ place and ready to begin oil production.
- (A) at
- (B) on
- (C) **in** ✅
- (D) for

> **Why (C):** "**in place**" = fixed expression meaning "positioned and ready."
> (A) "at place" is not a standard expression. (B) "on place" does not exist. (D) "for place" is incorrect.
