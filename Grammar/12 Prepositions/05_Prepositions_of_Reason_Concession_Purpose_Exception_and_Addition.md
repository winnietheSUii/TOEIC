# 05_Prepositions_of_Reason_Concession_Purpose_Exception_and_Addition

## 📌 Overview
This unit covers **5 categories** of high-yield TOEIC prepositions:
1. **Reason** — why something happened (because of, due to, owing to)
2. **Concession** — contrasting an expectation (despite, in spite of)
3. **Purpose** — what something is for (for)
4. **Exception** — what is excluded (except, aside from, barring)
5. **Addition** — what is added (in addition to, besides)

> ⚡ The #1 TOEIC Part 5 trap: **Conjunction vs Preposition** — these are all **prepositions** and must be followed by a **noun/noun phrase**, NOT a full clause (S+V).

---

## 📊 Reference Table — REASON

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| **because of** | due to (most common) | Because of the rain, we stayed inside. |
| **due to** | caused by (formal) | Due to the rise in fuel costs, prices increased. |
| **owing to** | because of (very formal) | Owing to weather conditions, the event was postponed. |
| **on account of** | as a result of | On account of the delay, the shipment arrived late. |

> ⚠️ **All of these + NOUN only!** Use "because / since / as" + S+V for clauses.

## 📊 Reference Table — CONCESSION

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| **despite** | although (+ noun) | Despite a poor economy, the shop has been doing well. |
| **in spite of** | although (+ noun) | In spite of difficulties, we persevered. |
| **with all** | despite all | With all the evidence, they denied it. |
| **notwithstanding** | despite (very formal) | Notwithstanding the risks, they proceeded. |
| **regardless of** | no matter what | Regardless of the cost, quality must be maintained. |

> ⚠️ **despite ≠ although** → despite + noun | although + S+V

## 🧠 Core Rules

### Rule 1 — Conjunction vs Preposition (CRITICAL)
```
PREPOSITION (+ noun):     despite / in spite of / due to / because of
CONJUNCTION (+ S+V):      although / even though / because / since

✅ "Despite the rain, we played."       (despite + noun)
✅ "Although it rained, we played."     (although + clause)
❌ "Despite it rained, we played."      (WRONG — despite cannot take S+V)
❌ "Although the rain, we played."      (WRONG — although needs a clause)
```

### Rule 2 — "apart from" = double meaning
```
EXCEPTION:  "Apart from one error, the report was perfect." (= except for)
ADDITION:   "Apart from English, she also speaks French."  (= in addition to)
→ Read the context to decide which meaning
```

### Rule 3 — "barring" = unless / except
```
"Barring any problems, we will finish on time."
= Unless there are problems, we will finish on time.
= Except if there are problems, we will finish on time.
```

### Rule 4 — "instead of" vs "in place of" vs "in lieu of"
```
All three mean "replacement" — "instead of" is most common
"in place of" and "in lieu of" are more formal (TOEIC loves formal)
```

## ⚠️ TOEIC Traps

| Trap | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| despite + clause | despite it rained | **despite** the rain / **although** it rained |
| in spite of + clause | in spite of we tried | **in spite of** our efforts / **even though** we tried |
| because of + clause | because of it rained | **because of** the rain / **because** it rained |
| barring vs unless | unless unexpected delays | **barring** unexpected delays (prep) / **unless** there are delays (conj) |
| besides = "except" | besides the bathroom = excluding | ❌ "besides" = **in addition to**, not "except" |

## 🎯 Exam Thinking Strategy
1. **Check what follows the blank:** Noun → preposition / Clause (S+V) → conjunction
2. **Reason?** → because of / due to / owing to + noun
3. **Contrast/although?** → despite / in spite of + noun
4. **Excluding?** → except for / aside from / barring + noun
5. **Adding?** → in addition to / besides / apart from + noun
6. **"even though" in choices + "despite" in choices?** → look if a clause or noun follows

## ✏️ Practice Questions

**Q1.** The staff is planning to hold a farewell gathering ___ an employee who served the company for over twenty years.
- (A) by
- (B) **for** ✅
- (C) to
- (D) from

> **Why (B):** "for" indicates purpose — the gathering is **for** the employee (intended to honor them).
> (A) "by" shows agent/method, not purpose. (C) "to" shows direction, not purpose of event. (D) "from" shows source/origin.

---

**Q2.** The manager said that any changes, ___ the proposal would be presented at a special meeting.
- (A) except
- (B) **barring** ✅
- (C) unless
- (D) because

> **Why (B):** "barring" = except for / unless there are — followed by a noun phrase "the proposal." It means "except for the proposal, any changes would be presented."
> (A) "except" works grammatically but "barring" fits the formal TOEIC register better. (C) "unless" is a conjunction — needs S+V, not just a noun. (D) "because" = reason, doesn't fit the meaning.

---

**Q3.** Interest rates are falling ___ a governmental push to raise all mortgage rates for middle-class homeowners.
- (A) **in spite of** ✅
- (B) according
- (C) even though
- (D) in addition

> **Why (A):** "in spite of" = concession preposition + noun phrase "a governmental push." Rates are falling DESPITE the push to raise them — a contrast.
> (B) "according" needs "to" (according to). (C) "even though" is a conjunction — needs S+V, but here it's followed by a noun phrase. (D) "in addition" needs "to" (in addition to) and doesn't fit the contrast meaning.
