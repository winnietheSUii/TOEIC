# 📌 Verbs in That-Clauses (Subjunctive-Like Structures)
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 06 — Verb Forms | Part 3 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Bare Infinitive in That-Clauses after Demand/Necessity Verbs*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> After certain **verbs**, **adjectives**, and **nouns** that express suggestion, request, demand, or necessity, the verb inside the `that-clause` must be in the **bare infinitive** (base form).
> This is the **mandative subjunctive** structure — extremely common in TOEIC Part 5.

---

## 📐 The Pattern

```
[Trigger word] + that + [Subject] + [bare infinitive] + ...
```

> *"The hotel manager requests that any meetings **be held** in the conference room."*
> → `requests` (trigger) + `that` + `be held` (bare infinitive, passive) ✅

> *"It is essential that the process **be completed** in a timely fashion."*
> → `essential` (trigger adjective) + `that` + `be completed` (bare infinitive, passive) ✅

## 📋 Trigger Words — Memorize These Lists

### 🔹 Trigger Verbs
| Verb | Example |
|------|---------|
| `suggest` | *suggest that she **apply*** |
| `propose` | *propose that the board **vote*** |
| `recommend` | *recommend that he **take** the course* |
| `request` | *request that staff **submit** reports* |
| `ask` | *ask that clients **arrive** early* |
| `require` | *require that all forms **be filled** in* |
| `demand` | *demand that the contract **be revised*** |
| `insist` | *insist that the bags **be replaced*** |
| `command` | *command that soldiers **stand** by* |
| `order` | *order that work **cease** immediately* |

### 🔹 Trigger Adjectives (used in `It is + adj + that`)
| Adjective | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| `imperative` | *It is imperative that he **contact** us.* |
| `essential` | *It is essential that we **remain** aware.* |
| `necessary` | *It is necessary that all staff **attend**.* |
| `important` | *It is important that the city **receive** funds.* |
| `vital` | *It is vital that the report **be submitted** on time.* |
| `urgent` | *It is urgent that action **be taken**.* |

### 🔹 Trigger Nouns
| Noun | Example |
|------|---------|
| `advice` | *My advice is that she **take** more classes.* |
| `recommendation` | *The recommendation is that staff **be trained**.* |
| `request` | *The request is that all members **attend**.* |
| `suggestion` | *The suggestion is that costs **be reduced**.* |
| `requirement` | *The requirement is that forms **be submitted** by Friday.* |

---

## ⚠️ Key Rule — No `-s`, No Tense Inflection, No `to`

> Inside the that-clause after a trigger:
> - ❌ No 3rd person `-s`: *she **takes** → she **take*** ✅
> - ❌ No present/past tense: *are held → **be** held* ✅
> - ❌ No `to-infinitive`: *to remain → **remain*** ✅
> - ❌ No future `will`: *will submit → **submit*** ✅

### Error Examples

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---------|---------|
| *"My advice is that she **takes** more classes."* | *"...she **take** more classes."* |
| *"requests that meetings **are** held..."* | *"...meetings **be** held..."* |
| *"imperative that you **will submit**..."* | *"...you **submit**..."* |
| *"essential that we **to remain**..."* | *"...we **remain**..."* |

---

## 🔑 Passive in That-Clauses

> When the that-clause verb is passive → use bare infinitive of `be` + past participle:
> **`be + past participle`** (not `are/is/was`)

> *"The manufacturer insists that the defective bags **be replaced**."* ✅
> *"...that the bags **are replaced**."* ❌ (cannot use `are` after `insists that`)

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — 3rd person `-s` in that-clause:** *"important that the city **receives**"* ❌ → `receive` ✅
- **Trap 2 — `will` inside that-clause:** *"imperative that you **will submit**"* ❌ → `submit` ✅
- **Trap 3 — `are/is` instead of `be` (passive):** *"insists that bags **are replaced**"* ❌ → `be replaced` ✅
- **Trap 4 — `to-infinitive` after trigger:** *"essential that we **to remain**"* ❌ → `remain` ✅

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Spot the trigger word — is it a verb (suggest/require/insist), adjective (essential/imperative), or noun (advice/recommendation)?
> **Step 2:** Confirm `that` follows the trigger word.
> **Step 3:** The verb in the that-clause → **bare infinitive** (no `-s`, no tense, no `to`)
> **Step 4:** Is the clause passive? → `be + past participle` (bare `be`, not `is/are`)
> **Step 5:** Eliminate: `receives` / `will receive` / `is received` / `to receive` → all wrong

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The Trattoria Restaurant requests that patrons _____ reservations for a table at least two weeks ahead of time."*

- (A) makes · (B) making · (C) made · **(D) make ✅**

> 💡 `requests that` = trigger → bare infinitive → `make`. `makes` = 3rd person `-s` ❌; `making` = gerund ❌; `made` = past tense ❌.

### Q2
> *"The manufacturer insists that the defective air bags _____ replaced in spite of the high cost of recalling thousands of affected cars."*

- (A) are · **(B) be ✅** · (C) have · (D) has

> 💡 `insists that` = trigger → bare infinitive in passive = `be` + past participle → `be replaced`. `are replaced` ❌ — the `be` must be bare (not inflected).

### Q3
> *"Due to the urgency of this matter, it is imperative that Mr. Lambert _____ one of our customer representatives by 5 p.m."*

- (A) is contacting · (B) will contact · **(C) contact ✅** · (D) contacted

> 💡 `it is imperative that` = trigger adjective → bare infinitive → `contact`. `will contact` ❌ (future tense); `is contacting` ❌ (progressive); `contacted` ❌ (past tense).

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| 3rd `-s` after trigger | Drop the `-s` | *"essential that everyone **attend**"* — no -s ever |
| `will + V` in that-clause | Remove `will` | The trigger already implies necessity — no future needed |
| `is/are` for passive | `be` (bare) | After trigger → passive = `be + p.p.` (bare `be`) |
| `to + V` in that-clause | Drop `to` | Bare infinitive = base form only — no `to` |

> ✏️ **Pattern Reminder:**
> `[suggest/require/insist/essential/important] + that + [Subject] + [BASE VERB]`
> → No `-s` · No tense · No `to` · No `will`
> → Passive = `be + past participle`
