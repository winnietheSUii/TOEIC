# 📌 Modal & Auxiliary Verbs + Bare Infinitive
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 06 — Verb Forms | Part 1 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: What Follows Modal & Auxiliary Verbs*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> After a **modal verb** or **auxiliary verb**, the main verb must be in the **bare infinitive** form — the base form with **no `to`**, no `-s`, no `-ed`, no `-ing`.
> This is one of the most frequently tested structures in TOEIC Part 5.

---

## 📂 Group 1 — Modal Verbs + Bare Infinitive

> **Modal verbs:** `will` · `would` · `may` · `might` · `can` · `could` · `must` · `should` · `shall`

> **Rule:** Modal verb → always followed by **bare infinitive** (no exceptions)

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Error Type |
|---------|---------|------------|
| *I will **to call** you.* | *I will **call** you.* | `to-infinitive` after modal |
| *We could **going** to the park.* | *We could **go** to the park.* | Gerund after modal |
| *She will **forgives** you.* | *She will **forgive** you.* | 3rd person `-s` added |
| *You should **remembrance** to bring items.* | *You should **remember** to bring items.* | Noun instead of verb |

---

## 📂 Group 2 — Auxiliary `do/does/did` + Bare Infinitive

> `do` / `does` / `did` are used to form negatives, questions, and inversion.
> The verb after `did not` / `did` (inversion) → always **bare infinitive**.

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Error Type |
|---------|---------|------------|
| *They did not **arrived** until 2 p.m.* | *They did not **arrive** until 2 p.m.* | Past tense after `did` |
| *Never did I **thought** about switching careers.* | *Never did I **think** about switching careers.* | Past tense in inversion |
| *Only on Saturday does the clinic **accepts** patients.* | *...does the clinic **accept** patients.* | 3rd person `-s` after `does` |

> ⚠️ `does` already carries the tense — never add `-s` to the verb after it.

---

## 📂 Group 3 — `had better` + Bare Infinitive

> `had better` = a strong recommendation / warning
> → always followed by **bare infinitive**, never `to-infinitive` or past form

> *"You had better **turn** in those evaluations before you request time off."* ✅
> *"You had better **turned**..."* ❌

---

## 🔑 All Triggers → Bare Infinitive

| Trigger | Example |
|---------|---------|
| `will / would / can / could` | *will **succeed*** |
| `may / might` | *might **arrive*** |
| `must / should / shall` | *must **establish*** |
| `do / does / did` (negative) | *did not **arrive*** |
| `do / does / did` (inversion) | *Never did I **think**…* |
| `had better` | *had better **turn*** |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `to-infinitive` after modal:** `will to call` ❌ → `will call` ✅ No `to` after modals
- **Trap 2 — 3rd person `-s` after modal/did:** `will succeeds` / `does accepts` ❌ → bare form only
- **Trap 3 — Past tense after `did`:** `did not arrived` ❌ → `did not arrive` ✅ (`did` already shows past)
- **Trap 4 — Gerund after modal:** `could going` ❌ → `could go` ✅
- **Trap 5 — Noun after modal:** `should remembrance` ❌ → `should remember` ✅

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Spot a modal or auxiliary verb before the blank.
> **Step 2:** The answer is automatically → **bare infinitive** (base form).
> **Step 3:** Eliminate:
> - `to + verb` (to-infinitive)
> - `verb + -ing` (gerund/participle)
> - `verb + -s` (3rd person present)
> - `verb + -ed` (past tense)
> - Any noun or adjective form

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The corporation's plan to overhaul the employee benefits program will _____ because it has the full support of the staff."*

- (A) success · **(B) succeed ✅** · (C) succeeds · (D) to succeed

> 💡 `will` = modal → bare infinitive → `succeed`. `success` = noun; `succeeds` = 3rd person; `to succeed` = to-infinitive.

### Q2
> *"To achieve success in today's competitive environment, companies must _____ a solid business plan."*

- (A) to establish · (B) establishing · **(C) establish ✅** · (D) establishes

> 💡 `must` = modal → bare infinitive → `establish`. Remove all extras.

### Q3
> *"The book...did not _____ until three weeks after the expected delivery date."*

- (A) arrival · **(B) arrive ✅** · (C) arriving · (D) arrived

> 💡 `did not` = auxiliary negative → bare infinitive → `arrive`. `arrived` ❌ — `did` already marks past tense.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| Modal + `to + verb` | Modal + bare verb | Modals are already "full" — no `to` needed |
| `did` + past tense | `did` + bare infinitive | `did` = past marker. Verb stays base form |
| `does` + `-s` verb | `does` + bare infinitive | `does` carries the 3rd person — verb drops the `-s` |
| `had better` + past | `had better` + bare | "had better" ≠ past — it's a warning expression |

> ✏️ **Golden Rule:**
> `Modal / do / does / did / had better` → **BARE INFINITIVE** (always, no exceptions)
