# 📌 Transitive vs. Intransitive Verbs
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 06 — Verb Forms | Part 2 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Object Structure & Ditransitive Verbs*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Every verb belongs to a structural type — it either **takes a direct object** (transitive) or **doesn't** (intransitive).
> TOEIC tests whether you choose the right verb for the sentence structure provided.

---

## 📂 Transitive vs. Intransitive — Core Difference

| Type | Takes direct object? | Needs preposition? | Example |
|------|---------------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Transitive (Vt)** | ✅ Yes — directly | ❌ No preposition | *"We discussed **the issue**."* |
| **Intransitive (Vi)** | ❌ No direct object | ✅ Needs preposition | *"We talked **about the issue**."* |

---

## 🔑 Rule — Intransitive + Preposition vs. Transitive

> If the sentence has a **preposition before the object**, the verb must be **intransitive**.
> If the object follows the verb **directly** (no preposition), use a **transitive** verb.

### ❌ Classic Error

> *"We have to **discuss about** the sudden decrease in export sales."*
> ❌ `discuss` = transitive — cannot take `about`
> ✅ `talk about` = intransitive (requires `about`)
> ✅ `discuss the decrease` = transitive (no preposition needed)

---

## 📋 Key Transitive vs. Intransitive Pairs

| Transitive (no preposition) | Intransitive (needs preposition) |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| `discuss` sth | `talk about` sth |
| `mention` sth | `refer to` sth |
| `reach` sth | `arrive at/in` sth |
| `contact` sb | `speak to/with` sb |
| `notify` sb | `mention to` sb |
| `oppose` sth | `object to` sth |
| `answer` sth | `reply to` sth |
| `attend` sth | `participate in` sth |
| `enter` sth | `go into` sth |
| `call` sb | `talk to` sb |

> ⚠️ `object to` — NOT `oppose to`. `object` is intransitive; `oppose` is transitive.
> ⚠️ `arrive at` the office / `arrive in` the city — never `arrive` + place directly.

---

## 📂 Ditransitive Verbs — Two Objects

> Some verbs take **two objects**: an **indirect object** (person) + a **direct object** (thing or that-clause).
> These are called **ditransitive verbs**.

> **Pattern:** `Subject + Verb + [Indirect Object] + [Direct Object / that-clause]`

| Ditransitive Verb | Structure | Example |
|-------------------|-----------|---------|
| `tell` | tell sb (that)... | *"The supplier **told** the purchaser that they had new stock."* |
| `inform` | inform sb (that)... | *"The Chief **informed** us that we should take precautions."* |
| `advise` | advise sb (that)... | *"The council **advised** the director that support might result in loss."* |
| `assure` | assure sb (that)... | *"He **assured** me that the project would succeed."* |
| `convince` | convince sb (that)... | *"She **convinced** us that the plan was viable."* |
| `remind` | remind sb (that/to)... | *"Please **remind** the team that the deadline is Friday."* |
| `notify` | notify sb (that)... | *"Please **notify** the attendees that the meeting is postponed."* |
| `warn` | warn sb (that/about)... | *"The manager **warned** staff that overtime would be required."* |

---

## ⚠️ Ditransitive vs. Non-Ditransitive

> Some similar-looking verbs **cannot** take two objects — they need a preposition.

| ❌ Cannot take indirect object directly | ✅ Correct structure |
|----------------------------------------|---------------------|
| `said to us that...` | → `told us that...` (`said` ≠ ditransitive) |
| `explained the director that...` | → `explained to the director that...` |
| `mentioned us that...` | → `mentioned to us that...` |
| `suggested him that...` | → `suggested to him that...` |
| `recommended her that...` | → `recommended to her that...` |

> 💡 **Key pattern:** `tell/inform/advise/assure/convince/notify/warn` → take indirect object **directly** (no `to`)
> `say/explain/mention/suggest/recommend` → need **`to` + person** before the that-clause

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `discuss about`:** `discuss` = transitive → no `about`. *"Let's **discuss** the problem."* ✅
- **Trap 2 — `said` vs `told`:** *"She said us that..."* ❌ → `said` cannot take indirect object → `told us that` ✅
- **Trap 3 — `explain to` vs `explained`:** Must have `to` before the person: *"explained **to** the director"* ✅
- **Trap 4 — `object to` not `oppose to`:** `object to` = intransitive fixed expression; `oppose` = transitive (no `to`)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Look at the sentence structure after the blank.
> **Step 2:** Is there a **preposition** (to/about/at) after the object? → Intransitive verb
> **Step 3:** Does the object follow the verb **directly** with no preposition? → Transitive verb
> **Step 4:** Are there **TWO objects** (person + thing/clause)? → Ditransitive verb needed
> **Step 5:** Check the list — does the verb take an indirect object directly, or does it need `to`?

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The Chief of Security _____ to us that we should take the highest precautions to ensure no information was leaked."*

- **(A) informed ✅** · (B) convinced · (C) notified · (D) mentioned

> 💡 `_____ to us that...` → indirect object (`to us`) + that-clause → ditransitive structure. `informed` ✅ naturally takes `informed us` OR `informed to us`. `convinced` → needs `of`-clause. `mentioned` → not a standard ditransitive here.

### Q2
> *"The council _____ the director that continued support of the Baker project might result in financial loss."*

- **(A) advised ✅** · (B) explained · (C) recommended · (D) announced

> 💡 `_____ the director that...` → direct indirect object (no `to`) + that-clause → ditransitive. `advised` ✅ takes indirect object directly. `explained/recommended/announced` all need `to` before the person.

### Q3
> *"My stockbroker _____ me about a safe investment plan that would secure my financial future."*

- (A) gave · **(B) told ✅** · (C) suggested · (D) recommended

> 💡 `_____ me about...` → indirect object (`me`) + prepositional phrase (`about...`). `told` ✅ — ditransitive taking `me` directly. `suggested/recommended` need `to me`. `gave` needs `to` or a different structure.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `discuss about` | `discuss` (no `about`) | `discuss` = transitive — object follows directly |
| `said us that` | `told us that` | `say` ≠ ditransitive; `tell` = ditransitive |
| `explained the director that` | `explained to the director that` | `explain` always needs `to` before the person |
| `object` without `to` | `object to` | `object` is always intransitive — always needs `to` |
| `arrive` + place | `arrive at/in` + place | `arrive` is intransitive — needs preposition |
