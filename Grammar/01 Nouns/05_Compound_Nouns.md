# 📌 Compound Nouns
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 01 — Nouns | Part 5 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Noun + Noun Structure & Pluralization*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> A **compound noun** = two or more words working together as one noun.
> TOEIC tests whether you can identify the correct word form in a compound noun structure.

---

## 📂 Structure of Compound Nouns

### Most Common Pattern: **Noun + Noun**

> `communication` + `skills` = **communication skills**
> `research` + `program` = **research program**
> `exchange` + `rate` = **exchange rate**

The **first word** functions like an **adjective** — it **modifies** the second noun.

> ⚠️ The modifier (first word) **does NOT change form**:
> - It never takes a plural `-s`
> - It stays in its base noun form, NOT a verb or adjective form

---

## 🔑 Pluralization Rule

> **Only the SECOND noun gets `-s`** to form a plural compound noun.

| Singular | Plural |
|---|---|
| research program | research programs |
| conference room | conference rooms |
| reference letter | reference letters |

> ❌ "two *thousands* tickets" → ✅ "two **thousand** tickets"
> (Number words used as modifiers don't take `-s`)

---

## ⚠️ Common Error Example

> ❌ *"Good communicated skills are essential to the project."*
> ✅ *"Good communication skills are essential to the project."*
> → The modifier must be a **noun** form (`communication`), not a past participle (`communicated`).

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Verb form as modifier:** "communicated skills" ❌ → modifier must be a **noun**, not a past participle
- **Trap 2 — Adjective form as modifier:** "exercisable equipment" ❌ → `exercise equipment` ✅
- **Trap 3 — Plural modifier:** "two thousands tickets" ❌ → "two thousand tickets" ✅ (number words as modifiers stay singular)
- **Trap 4 — `-ing` form:** "exercising equipment" ❌ → The `-ing` form here is a participle, not the noun modifier. Always choose the base noun form.

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Look at the blank's position — is it **before another noun**?
> **Step 2:** If yes → the blank is the **modifier** in a compound noun → must be a **noun form** (base form).
> **Step 3:** Reject verb forms (-ed, -ing), adjective forms (-able, -ive), and adverb forms (-ly).
> **Step 4:** For pluralization — `several` or other quantifiers before the compound → only pluralize the **last word**.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The _____ program is being financed privately, although the government is looking into the possibility of supporting the program."*

- (A) researching
- (B) researched
- **(C) research ✅**
- (D) researches

> 💡 `_____ program` → blank is the **modifier** in a compound noun → must be base noun form → `research`. `-ing` and `-ed` are verb/participle forms.

---

### Q2
> *"The _____ equipment at the new fitness club is guaranteed to help men and women achieve greater muscle tone..."*

- **(A) exercise ✅**
- (B) exercisable
- (C) exercising
- (D) exercised

> 💡 `_____ equipment` → compound noun → modifier must be base noun form → `exercise`. `exercisable` is adjective; `exercising/exercised` are verb forms.

---

### Q3
> *"With nearly two _____ orders placed within the first day, the manager has decided to add another work shift..."*

- (A) thousands
- (B) a thousand
- **(C) thousand ✅**
- (D) thousands of

> 💡 "two _____ orders" → number word as modifier → stays **singular** (no `-s`). `thousands of` would need to stand alone without `two`.
> The pattern: `two thousand` + noun ✅; `thousands of` + noun ✅ (but not with `two`)

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---|---|---|
| `communicated skills` | `communication skills` | Modifier = always noun (not verb) |
| `exercising equipment` | `exercise equipment` | The `-ing` here acts as a verb, not a noun |
| `two thousands tickets` | `two thousand tickets` | Numbers as modifiers = no `-s` (twelve *month* contract) |
| `researches program` | `research program` | Modifier noun = singular base form always |

> ✏️ **Golden Rule:** In `[Modifier Noun] + [Head Noun]`:
> - Modifier stays **base form** (no `-s`, no `-ed`, no `-ing`)
> - Only the **head noun** (last one) can be pluralized
