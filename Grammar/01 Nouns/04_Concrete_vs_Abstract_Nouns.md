# 📌 Concrete Nouns vs. Abstract Nouns
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 01 — Nouns | Part 4 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Meaning-Based Noun Selection*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Both types fill noun positions, but the **meaning of the sentence** determines which type fits.
> TOEIC tests your ability to distinguish a **person** noun from a **concept/process** noun.

---

## 📂 The Two Types

### 👤 Concrete Nouns
- Refer to things you can **perceive with your 5 senses**
- Includes **people, physical objects, places**
- Examples: `architect`, `consultant`, `employee`, `table`, `salt`

### 💡 Abstract Nouns
- Refer to things that **cannot be seen or touched**
- Includes **ideas, processes, states, concepts**
- Examples: `architecture`, `consultation`, `employment`, `satisfaction`

---

## 🔑 How to Choose in TOEIC

Ask yourself:
> **"Does the sentence need a PERSON or a PROCESS/CONCEPT?"**

| Sentence Context | Noun Type Needed |
|---|---|
| "serve as ___" / "work as ___" | Person (concrete) |
| "the ___ of the company" / "long-term ___" | Concept/Process (abstract) |
| Subject that performs an action | Person (concrete) |
| Subject that is a situation or state | Abstract |

---

## ⚠️ Common Error Examples

### Error 1 — Using abstract when person is needed
> ❌ *"An **architecture** carefully studies a site before making a perspective."*
> ✅ *"An **architect** carefully studies a site before making a perspective."*
> → The subject does the action of studying → needs a **person** = `architect`

---

### Error 2 — Using person when concept is needed
> ❌ *"The **founder** of the local art center reflects the city's commitment to culture."*
> ✅ *"The **foundation** of the local art center reflects the city's commitment to culture."*
> → This sentence talks about an organization's basis → needs an abstract noun = `foundation`

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — "serve as ___":** Always needs a **person** noun. "serve as a *consultation*" ❌ → "serve as a *consultant*" ✅
- **Trap 2 — "the ___ of [organization]":** Usually needs an **abstract** noun. "the *employee* of the company" (can work) vs "the *employment* rate" (process)
- **Trap 3 — Two noun options, both grammatically correct:** Choose based on **meaning**. This is the hardest type — slow down and read the full sentence.
- **Trap 4 — `-or` vs `-tion`:** Both end a blank correctly, but completely different meanings.

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Confirm the blank is in a noun position (check signal words).
> **Step 2:** Read the **full sentence** for meaning — does it need a person or a concept?
> **Step 3:** Look for context clues:
> - *"serve as / work as / hire a"* → **person**
> - *"long-term / the ___ of / result in"* → **concept/process**
> **Step 4:** Use suffix knowledge to classify options quickly.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"If long-term _____ is a possibility at this company, I would be interested and willing to relocate..."*

- (A) employ
- **(B) employment ✅**
- (C) employee
- (D) employing

> 💡 `long-term ___` = a situation/concept → needs abstract noun. `employment` = the state of being employed. `employee` = a person → doesn't fit "long-term possibility."

---

### Q2
> *"The chemical company gave local government officials a report detailing the _____ of its plant in Westbury."*

- (A) operative
- (B) operator
- **(C) operation ✅**
- (D) operate

> 💡 "detailing the _____ of its plant" → the **process/activity** of the plant, not a person. `operation` = how the plant runs. `operator` = a person.

---

### Q3
> *"...for psychologists to serve as _____ to toy companies in the design of toys..."*

- (A) consult
- **(B) consultant ✅**
- (C) consultation
- (D) consulting

> 💡 "serve as ___" → always needs a **person** noun. `consultant` = a person who advises. `consultation` = the act of consulting.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---|---|---|
| `architect` chosen for a concept | Use `architecture` | "The city's *architecture*" = style/concept |
| `employment` vs `employee` | Context: concept vs person | `-ment` = state; `-ee` = the person receiving action |
| `operator` vs `operation` | Context: person vs process | `-or` = person who operates; `-tion` = the activity |
| `consultant` vs `consultation` | "serve as" = person | "serve as a *consultant*" (not a process) |

> ✏️ **Suffix Cheat Sheet:**
> `-er / -or / -ist / -ant / -ee` = **PERSON**
> `-tion / -ment / -ance / -ure / -ity` = **CONCEPT**
