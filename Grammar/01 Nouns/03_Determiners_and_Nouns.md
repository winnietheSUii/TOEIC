# 📌 Determiners and Nouns
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 01 — Nouns | Part 3 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Articles & Quantifier Matching*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> **Determiners** are words placed before a noun to clarify *which one*, *how many*, or *how much*.
> In TOEIC, you must match the right determiner to the right type of noun.

---

## 📂 Types of Determiners

### 1️⃣ Articles

| Article | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `a` | Singular count noun only | *a company*, *a meeting* |
| `an` | Singular count noun starting with vowel sound | *an office*, *an hour* |
| `the` | Any noun (count or non-count, specific) | *the report*, *the equipment* |

> ⚠️ `a / an` **CANNOT** be used with:
> - Plural nouns: *~~a prices~~* ❌
> - Non-count nouns: *~~an information~~* ❌

---

### 2️⃣ Quantifiers — Full Reference Table

| Quantifier | Singular Count | Plural Count | Non-Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| `one` | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| `every` | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| `each` | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| `many` | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| `several` | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| `fewer` | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| `a few` | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| `much` | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| `less` | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| `a little` | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| `some` | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |

| `most` | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| `any` | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

---

## 🔑 Key Rules

1. **`a / an`** → only with **singular count** nouns
2. **`much`** → only with **non-count** nouns
3. **`many` / `several` / `fewer`** → only with **plural count** nouns → noun **must** end in `-s`
4. **`each` / `every`** → only with **singular count** nouns → noun **must NOT** end in `-s`
5. **`the`** → works with any noun (count or non-count)

---

## ⚠️ Common Errors with Examples

### Error 1 — `much` with plural noun
> ❌ *"Much financial advisors will charge an hourly rate."*
> ✅ *"Many financial advisors will charge an hourly rate."*
> → `advisors` = plural count → use `many`

---

### Error 2 — `several` with singular noun
> ❌ *"...offers customers several convenient new feature."*
> ✅ *"...offers customers several convenient new **features**."*
> → `several` demands plural → noun must be `features`

---

### Error 3 — `a / an` with non-count noun
> ❌ *"I've got so interesting **an** information about the event."*
> ✅ *"I've got some interesting **information** about the event."*
> → `information` is non-count → no `a / an`

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `each` requires singular:** "each *employees*" → ❌ → `each employee`
- **Trap 2 — `several/many` with missing `-s`:** Quantifier is correct but noun isn't pluralized
- **Trap 3 — `Some` with non-count:** Correct! `some equipment` ✅ — students often doubt this
- **Trap 4 — Blank = quantifier, not noun:** Sometimes the blank is before the noun → pick the right quantifier, not a noun form

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Identify what is being tested — is the **blank the noun** or the **determiner**?
> **Step 2 (If blank = noun):** Look at the determiner before it → match singular/plural/non-count.
> **Step 3 (If blank = determiner):** Identify the noun after → singular, plural, or non-count → choose matching quantifier.
> **Step 4:** Eliminate options that violate the grammar rule immediately.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"_____ merchandise returned to the store after purchase must be checked for flaws..."*

- (A) An
- (B) There is
- **(C) Some ✅**
- (D) The

> 💡 `merchandise` = non-count. `An` ❌ (can't use with non-count). `Some` ✅ works with non-count. `The` would also work grammatically, but the sentence implies *any* returned merchandise, not a specific one → `Some` fits best.

---

### Q2
> *"...a program that offers customers several convenient new _____"*

- (A) to feature
- (B) feature
- (C) featuring
- **(D) features ✅**

> 💡 `several` = plural count → must use `features` (plural). All other options are verb forms.

---

### Q3
> *"A probationary accounting trainee must undergo six months' instruction before _____ is allowed to work..."*

- (A) All
- (B) Much
- **(C) Each ✅**
- (D) A few

> 💡 The sentence talks about **one trainee** (singular) → `each` (singular) matches. `All` and `A few` imply multiple people. `Much` is for non-count nouns.

---

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Root Cause | Remember This |
|---|---|---|
| `much advisors` | Confusion of quantifier type | `much` = non-count only (water, money, time) |
| `several feature` | Forgot to pluralize | If `several` → always check for `-s` on noun |
| `an information` | Treated non-count as count | Information = cannot be counted, like water |
| `each employees` | `each` = always singular | `each` + singular noun (no `-s`) |

> ✏️ **Memory Rhyme:** *"Many/Several/Fewer = count, need an `s` | Much/Less/A little = no count, no `s`"*
