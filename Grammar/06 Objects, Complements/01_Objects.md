# 📌 Objects — Forms & Position
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 07 — Objects & Complements | Part 1 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: What Can Be an Object & What Cannot*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> An **object** receives the action of a transitive verb.
> It always functions like a **noun** — the form may vary, but the grammatical role is the same.
> TOEIC tests whether you can identify what belongs in an object slot and avoid placing verbs or adjectives there.

---

## 📋 Valid Object Forms

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Noun phrase** | *"Jake has enough business sense to run **his own company**."* |
| **Pronoun** | *"I can't find **them** anywhere in the office."* |
| **Gerund phrase** | *"Fred hates **working overtime**."* |
| **To-infinitive phrase** | *"Helen wants **to get a new job**."* |
| **Noun clause** | *"I agree **that we need new filing cabinets**."* |

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — No Verbs or Adjectives in Object Position

> Only noun-like forms can be objects.
> **Verbs** and **adjectives** must be converted to their noun form before filling an object slot.

### ❌ Error Examples

| Wrong | Right | Problem |
|-------|-------|---------|
| *"The company guarantees **deliver** in five to seven days."* | *"...guarantees **delivery**..."* | `deliver` = verb → needs noun form |
| *"Planners designed the **productive** with the teenage consumer in mind."* | *"...designed the **product**..."* | `productive` = adjective → needs noun form |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Verb in object slot:** `"takes great **please** in..."` ❌ → `pleasure` (noun) ✅
- **Trap 2 — Adjective in object slot:** `"formulate a **strategic**"` ❌ → `strategy` (noun) ✅
- **Trap 3 — Wrong noun (process vs. result):** `"know the **proceed**"` ❌ → `procedures` (noun, plural) ✅
- **Trap 4 — Singular vs. plural noun:** Match the object's number to the context (e.g., `company tours` = plural → `procedures` not `procedure`)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the verb before the blank. Is it transitive? → It needs an **object**.
> **Step 2:** The object slot must be filled by a **noun** (or noun equivalent).
> **Step 3:** Eliminate:
> - Verbs (`please`, `deliver`, `proceed`)
> - Adjectives (`pleasant`, `productive`, `strategic`)
> **Step 4:** Among noun options — choose by **meaning and number** (singular/plural).

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Reliance Property Developers takes great _____ in announcing that we have purchased land in the Hartford area."*

- (A) please · **(B) pleasure ✅** · (C) pleasant · (D) pleasurable

> 💡 `takes great _____` → object of `takes` → **noun** → `pleasure`. `please` = verb; `pleasant/pleasurable` = adjectives.

### Q2
> *"Working in the field helped the trainees to formulate a better _____ that would address the problems raised by Mr. White."*

- (A) strategic · (B) strategical · **(C) strategy ✅** · (D) strategize

> 💡 `formulate a better _____` → object → **noun** → `strategy`. Both (A) and (B) are adjectives; (D) is a verb.

### Q3
> *"The manager posted a note stating that the staff should know the revised _____ for company tours."*

- (A) proceed · (B) proceeds · (C) procedural · **(D) procedures ✅**

> 💡 `know the revised _____` → object → **noun**. Context: `company tours` = plural → `procedures` (plural noun). `proceed` = verb; `procedural` = adjective; `proceeds` = 3rd person verb form.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| Verb in object slot | Convert to noun | `deliver` → `delivery`; `comply` → `compliance` |
| Adjective in object slot | Convert to noun | `productive` → `product`; `strategic` → `strategy` |
| Wrong noun number | Match context | Plural context → plural noun (`tours` → `procedures`) |
| Process noun vs. person noun | Read meaning | `operation` (process) ≠ `operator` (person) |
