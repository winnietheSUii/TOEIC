# 📌 Complements — Forms & Types
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 07 — Objects & Complements | Part 3 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Subject Complement vs. Object Complement*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> A **complement** completes the meaning of the subject or object.
> Complements can be **nouns** (identifying who/what) or **adjectives** (describing a quality).
> **Verbs and adverbs** can never fill a complement slot.

---

## 📋 Valid Complement Forms

| Form | Type | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| **Noun phrase** | Subject complement | *"Previous experience will be **an advantage**."* |
| **Gerund phrase** | Subject complement | *"His specialty is **designing databases**."* |
| **To-infinitive phrase** | Subject complement | *"The important thing is **to include your phone number**."* |
| **Noun clause** | Subject complement | *"The problem is **that we don't have sufficient capital**."* |
| **Adjective** | Subject complement | *"The new investments are **profitable**."* |
| **Adjective** | Object complement | *"We found the art exhibit **fascinating**."* |

---

## 📂 Two Types of Complements

### ✅ Subject Complement — describes or identifies the **subject**
> Comes after a **linking verb**: `be/become/get/seem/remain/turn/taste/feel/look/sound`

> *"She became **a professional musician**."*  (noun — identifies her)
> *"The prices were quite **competitive**."*  (adjective — describes prices)

---

### ✅ Object Complement — describes or identifies the **object**
> Comes after verbs like `make/keep/find/consider/call`

> *"Mr. Shaw considered himself **a self-motivated individual**."*  (noun — identifies him)
> *"The bank keeps its depositors **satisfied**."*  (adjective — describes depositors)

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — No Verbs or Adverbs as Complements

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Error |
|---------|---------|-------|
| *"All employees are **cooperate** with the new policy."* | *"...are **cooperative**."* | Verb → needs adjective |
| *"Made the performance **excitingly**."* | *"...made the performance **exciting**."* | Adverb → needs adjective |

> ✅ Complements accept: **Noun** or **Adjective** only.
> ❌ Never: **Verb**, **Adverb**, or any other class.

---

## 📋 Linking Verbs → Subject Complement

| Verb | Example |
|------|---------|
| `be` | *It **is** important.* |
| `become` | *She **became** a manager.* |
| `seem` | *He **seems** qualified.* |
| `remain` | *The situation **remains** tense.* |
| `get` | *Things will **get** better.* |
| `turn` | *The milk **turned** sour.* |
| `look` | *The report **looks** complete.* |
| `feel` | *The team **feels** confident.* |
| `sound` | *The plan **sounds** reasonable.* |
| `taste` | *The coffee **tastes** bitter.* |

---

## 📋 Object Complement Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|------|---------|
| `make` | *This **makes** us **nervous**.* |
| `find` | *We **found** it **fascinating**.* |
| `keep` | *This **keeps** staff **motivated**.* |
| `consider` | *We **consider** him **an expert**.* |
| `call` | *People **called** him **a leader**.* |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Adverb after linking verb:** `"are **cooperatively**"` ❌ → `cooperative` (adjective) ✅
- **Trap 2 — Verb after linking verb:** `"are **cooperate**"` ❌ → `cooperative` ✅
- **Trap 3 — Noun vs. adjective as complement:** Both are valid — choose by meaning. Identity? → noun. Quality/state? → adjective
- **Trap 4 — `importance` vs. `important` after `is`:** `It is **importance**` ❌ → `It is **important**` ✅ (adjective needed after `is` for description)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Is the blank after a **linking verb** (is/are/seem/become)? → **Subject complement** (noun or adjective)
> **Step 2:** Is the blank after a **verb + object** (make/find/keep/consider)? → **Object complement** (noun or adjective)
> **Step 3:** Eliminate: verbs (`cooperate/disappoint`) and adverbs (`excitingly/importantly`)
> **Step 4:** If both noun and adjective remain → does the blank **identify** or **describe**? Identify → noun. Describe/evaluate → adjective.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"It was a great _____ that Brian Mann did not receive the Employee of the Year Award."*

- **(A) disappointment ✅** · (B) disappointed · (C) disappointing · (D) disappoint

> 💡 `It was a great _____` → subject complement after `was` → **noun** (after `a great`) → `disappointment`. `disappointed/disappointing` = adjectives (cannot follow `a great`); `disappoint` = verb.

### Q2
> *"Because prices at department store sales were quite _____, shoppers did not think it necessary to compare prices."*

- (A) competition · **(B) competitive ✅** · (C) competitor · (D) compete

> 💡 `were quite _____` → subject complement after `were` → **adjective** (modified by `quite`) → `competitive`. `competition/competitor` = nouns (cannot follow `quite`); `compete` = verb.

### Q3
> *"It is _____ for investors to check a company's performance before purchasing any shares."*

- (A) importance · **(B) important ✅** · (C) importantly · (D) import

> 💡 `It is _____` → subject complement after `is` → **adjective** → `important`. `importance` = noun; `importantly` = adverb; `import` = verb/noun with different meaning.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| Adverb after linking verb | Adjective | Linking verbs → adjective, NEVER adverb |
| Verb after linking verb | Adjective | `are cooperative` ✅ / `are cooperate` ❌ |
| `importance` after `is` | `important` | `It is important` = fixed evaluative structure |
| Forgetting object complements | After `make/find/keep/consider + object` | Object complement describes the object |
