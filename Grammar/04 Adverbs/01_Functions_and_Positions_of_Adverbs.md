# 📌 Functions and Positions of Adverbs
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 04 — Adverbs | Part 1 of 8**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Adverb Placement & Word Form*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> An **adverb** modifies a verb, adjective, another adverb, or an entire sentence.
> In TOEIC Part 5, you must know **where** adverbs go AND recognize that **only adverbs** can fill adverb positions — not adjectives.

---

## 📍 Position 1 — Adverb Modifying a Verb

| Placement | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Before verb + object** | Adverb comes before the whole verb phrase | *Mr. Fields **promptly** informed his supervisor.* |
| **After verb + object** | Adverb comes after the full verb phrase | *Filter each complaint **individually**.* |
| **Between auxiliary + main verb** | Slot between helping verb and main verb | *They are **still** arranging the office.* |
| **After modal/auxiliary** | Slot right after the modal | *His retirement has been **indefinitely** postponed.* |
| **After main verb "be"** | Adverb follows `be` directly | *The new assistant is adjusting **nicely**.* |

> ⚠️ **Never** place an adverb **between a verb and its direct object.**
> ❌ *He informed **quickly** his supervisor.* → ✅ *He **quickly** informed his supervisor.*

---

## 📍 Position 2 — Adverb Modifying an Adjective, Adverb, or Phrase

> **Rule:** The adverb comes **directly before** what it modifies.

| Modifies | Example |
|---|---|
| Adjective | *He gave an **exceptionally** profound speech.* |
| Another adverb | *She speaks English **very** well.* |
| Phrase | *The service operates **only** on the dates listed below.* |
| Superlative phrase | *The most **frequently** asked questions.* |

---

## 📍 Position 3 — Adverb Modifying a Clause or Sentence

| Placement | Example |
|---|---|
| **Beginning of sentence** | ***Unfortunately**, I won't be able to attend.* |
| **End of sentence** | *They will finish the survey **tomorrow**.* |
| **Beginning of clause** | *Children are admitted **only if** they are accompanied by an adult.* |

---

## ⚠️ The Core Rule — Adjectives CANNOT Be Adverbs

> **Only an adverb can fill an adverb position.**
> If the blank modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb → choose the **adverb form** (`-ly`), not the adjective.

### ❌ Error Examples

| Wrong | Right | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| *...have been cut **substantial**.* | *...cut **substantially**.* | Modifies verb `cut` → needs adverb |
| *...is **entire** up to you.* | *...**entirely** up to you.* | Modifies phrase `up to you` → needs adverb |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Adjective in adverb slot:** `technical` vs `technically` → adverb modifies an adjective (`impossible`) = **technically** ✅
- **Trap 2 — Comparative adjective:** `easier` looks like it could intensify — but it's still an adjective, not an adverb
- **Trap 3 — Adverb modifying a noun:** Adverbs CANNOT modify nouns. `shortly office etiquette` ❌ → use adjective `short` ✅
- **Trap 4 — Noun in adverb slot:** `technicality` and `technician` are nouns — never adverbs

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Look at what the blank is **next to** — a verb? adjective? noun?
> **Step 2:** If next to a **verb, adjective, or adverb** → blank needs an **adverb** (`-ly` form).
> **Step 3:** If next to a **noun** → blank needs an **adjective**, NOT an adverb.
> **Step 4:** Reject nouns (`-tion`, `-ity`, `-er`) and adjectives (`-al`, `-ive`, `-able`) for adverb slots.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"...it may be _____ impossible to lay the foundation for the memorial..."*

- **(A) technically ✅**
- (B) technician
- (C) technicality
- (D) technical

> 💡 Blank modifies the **adjective** `impossible` → must be an adverb → `technically`.

---

### Q2
> *"...mobile phone owners to transmit _____ clear and sharp images..."*

- (A) easy
- (B) easier
- (C) easiest
- **(D) easily ✅**

> 💡 Blank modifies the **adjectives** `clear and sharp` → adverb required → `easily`.

---

### Q3
> *"...a heated debate about _____ office etiquette."*

- **(A) short ✅**
- (B) shorter
- (C) shortly
- (D) short-lived

> 💡 Blank modifies the **noun** `office etiquette` → needs an **adjective**, not an adverb. `shortly` (adverb) cannot modify a noun.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---|---|---|
| Using adjective to modify a verb | Use `-ly` adverb | Verb + adverb: *runs quickly*, *cut substantially* |
| Using adverb to modify a noun | Use adjective | Nouns need adjectives, not adverbs |
| Putting adverb between verb and object | Move it before or after | *He **quickly** read the report.* ✅ |
| `technical` vs `technically` | `-ly` = adverb | If modifying adj/verb/adverb → add `-ly` |

> ✏️ **Quick Test:** What does the blank modify?
> - Verb / Adj / Adverb → **Adverb** (`-ly`)
> - Noun → **Adjective** (no `-ly`)
