# 📌 Subjects — Forms, Rules & Common Errors
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 08 — Subjects & Verbs | Part 1 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: What Can Be a Subject & Subject-Verb Number Agreement*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> The **subject** tells us *who* or *what* performs the action (or is the topic) of the sentence.
> It always comes **before the main verb**.
> TOEIC tests two things: (1) what forms can be subjects, and (2) subject-verb number agreement.

---

## 📋 Valid Subject Forms

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Noun phrase** | *"**The top executives** agreed to negotiate with the union."* |
| **Pronoun** | *"**They** are having a team meeting today."* |
| **Gerund phrase** | *"**Operating the machinery** is difficult."* |
| **To-infinitive phrase** | *"**To meet the president** was a privilege."* *(rare — usually replaced by Dummy Subject It)* |
| **Noun clause** | *"**What Jane needs for her office** is unclear."* |

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — Only Noun-Like Forms Can Be Subjects

> **Verbs** and **adjectives** cannot be subjects. They must first be converted to their noun form.

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Problem |
|---------|---------|---------|
| *"A rushed **decide** should be avoided."* | *"A rushed **decision** should be avoided."* | `decide` = verb → `decision` |
| *"The financial **healthy** of the company is in doubt."* | *"The financial **health** of the company..."* | `healthy` = adjective → `health` |

## 🔑 Subject-Verb Number Agreement

> The subject and verb must **match in number** — singular subject → singular verb; plural subject → plural verb.
> **Tip:** Don't be distracted by words between the subject and verb — focus on the head noun.

### ❌ Error Example
> *"The **prospects** of increased revenue **awaits** us next year."*
> ❌ `awaits` = singular · `prospects` = plural → ✅ `await`

> *"The **results** of the inquiry **was** made public today."*
> ❌ `was` = singular · `results` = plural → ✅ `were`

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Verb in subject slot:** "A rushed `decide`..." ❌ → `decision` ✅
- **Trap 2 — Adjective in subject slot:** "The financial `healthy`..." ❌ → `health` ✅
- **Trap 3 — Distractor between subject and verb:** "The **prospects** of revenue **awaits**" ❌ → `await` (subject = `prospects`, not `revenue`)
- **Trap 4 — Verb/noun same form:** `offer/deal/review` — look for article before blank to confirm noun role

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Is the blank before the main verb (no other subject before it)? → **Subject** slot → needs **noun**.
> **Step 2:** Find the verb that follows → what number/form is it? → Match the subject to it.
> **Step 3:** Eliminate verbs and adjectives from options.
> **Step 4:** For number agreement: ignore any prepositional phrase between subject and verb (`of the...`) and focus on the **head noun** only.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Please remember that the _____ of electronic devices is prohibited during takeoffs and landings."*

- **(A) operation ✅** · (B) operate · (C) operated · (D) operator

> 💡 `the _____ of...` → subject before `is` → **noun** → `operation` (the act). `operator` = person; `operate/operated` = verb forms.

### Q2
> *"Personal _____ left behind on seats in the theater are brought to the lost and found department."*

- (A) belonging · (B) belongs · **(C) belongings ✅** · (D) belong

> 💡 Verb = `are brought` (plural) → subject must be **plural noun** → `belongings` (plural noun = "personal items"). `belonging/belong` = verb forms.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| Verb in subject slot | Convert to noun | `decide` → `decision`; `apply` → `application` |
| Adjective in subject slot | Convert to noun | `healthy` → `health`; `productive` → `productivity` |
| Distractor phrase misleads number | Identify the HEAD noun | Ignore `of the...` — look at the main subject word |
| Plural verb with singular subject | Check head noun carefully | "The **number** of people IS..." (singular); "A **number** of people ARE..." (plural) |
