# 📌 Dummy Subject "There"
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 08 — Subjects & Verbs | Part 3 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: There + Linking Verb + Real Subject (Existence)*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> **Dummy subject `there`** signals **existence** — it tells us that something exists or is present.
> Structure: `There + linking verb (be/remain/exist) + real subject (noun phrase)`
> The **real subject** comes after the linking verb and determines the verb's number.

---

## 📐 The Pattern

```
There + linking verb + real subject (noun phrase)
                          ↑
             This determines singular/plural verb
```

---

## 📋 Examples

| Example | Real Subject | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| *"**There are** eleven offices on this floor."* | `eleven offices` (plural) | `are` = plural |
| *"**There remains** some doubt over her dedication."* | `some doubt` (singular) | `remains` = singular |
| *"**There exists** a possibility that the company will be bought out."* | `a possibility` (singular) | `exists` = singular |

---

## 🔑 `It` vs. `There` — The Key Distinction

| Dummy `It` | Dummy `There` |
|-----------|--------------|
| Expresses **opinion, fact, or evaluation** | Expresses **existence** of something |
| Followed by adjective/noun/prep phrase | Followed by **noun phrase only** |
| *"It is a good idea to plan ahead."* | *"There is a guidebook in each language."* |

> Test: Can you rephrase as *"[something] exists/is present"*? → Use `there`.
> Can you rephrase as *"it is [adj] that/to..."*? → Use `it`.

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — `There` Must Be Followed by a Noun

> After `There + linking verb`, only a **noun or noun phrase** is valid.
> Verb forms, adjectives, and adverbs cannot fill this slot.

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---------|---------|
| *"We will contact you if there are **cancelled**."* | *"...if there are **cancellations**."* |

---

## ⚠️ Number Agreement — Verb Matches Real Subject

> The linking verb (`be/remain/exist`) must agree in number with the **real subject**, not with `there`.

| Real Subject | Verb Form |
|-------------|-----------|
| Singular noun | `is / was / remains / exists` |
| Plural noun | `are / were / remain / exist` |

> *"There **are** errors of fact..."* → `errors` = plural → `are` ✅
> *"There **is** a guidebook..."* → `guidebook` = singular → `is` ✅

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `it` vs. `there`:** `"There is possible that..."` ❌ → possible = adjective → opinion → `"It is possible that..."` ✅
- **Trap 2 — Verb after `there are`:** `"there are cancelled"` ❌ → `there are cancellations` ✅ (noun required)
- **Trap 3 — Number mismatch:** `"there are a guidebook"` ❌ → `"there is a guidebook"` ✅
- **Trap 4 — Adverb instead of noun:** `"there was erroneously"` ❌ → `"there were errors"` ✅

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Does the sentence talk about **something existing/being present**? → `there`
> **Step 2:** Is the blank at the start + followed by `is/are/remain/exist` + noun? → `there`
> **Step 3:** After `there + linking verb` → blank must be a **noun or noun phrase**
> **Step 4:** Check verb number — match it to the **real subject** noun that follows

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"_____ remain significant challenges for the fledgling company as it tries to gain a foothold in a highly competitive market."*

- (A) It · **(B) There ✅** · (C) He · (D) What

> 💡 Sentence talks about `challenges` existing → **existence** → `there`. `It remain...` ❌ (`it` is for opinions). Real subject = `significant challenges` (plural) → `remain` ✅.

### Q2
> *"The publisher prints an apology when there are _____ of fact in previous issues."*

- (A) error · **(B) errors ✅** · (C) erring · (D) erroneously

> 💡 `there are _____` → noun required after `are`. `are` = plural → **plural noun** → `errors`. `erring` = participle; `erroneously` = adverb; `error` = singular (mismatch with `are`).

### Q3
> *"I am hopeful that somewhere in the city _____ exists a position with the hours and benefits I seek."*

- (A) it · **(B) there ✅** · (C) he · (D) what

> 💡 Inverted structure: `somewhere in the city there exists a position` → existence of a position → `there`. `it exists a position` ❌ — `it` doesn't introduce a real subject after `exist`.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `there is possible that` | `it is possible that` | `possible` = adjective → opinion → `it` |
| `there are cancelled` | `there are cancellations` | `there + are` → noun/noun phrase ONLY |
| `there are a guidebook` | `there is a guidebook` | Real subject is singular → singular verb |
| `it remain challenges` | `there remain challenges` | Existence of things → always `there` |
