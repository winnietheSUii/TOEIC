# 📌 Dummy Subject "It"
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 08 — Subjects & Verbs | Part 2 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: It as Placeholder Subject + Cannot Be Replaced by That/There*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> **Dummy subject `it`** is used when the real subject is long (a to-infinitive phrase or a that-clause).
> `it` holds the subject position while the real subject moves to the end.
> TOEIC tests both the structure itself and whether you can identify that only `it` (not `that` or `there`) works here.

---

## 📐 The Pattern

```
It + linking verb + adjective/noun/prepositional phrase + Real Subject
↑
dummy subject                                              ↑
                                               to-infinitive phrase
                                               OR that-clause
```

---

## 📋 Common Structures

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| `It + is + adj + to-infinitive` | *"**It** is important **to open negotiations** with the Brazilian firm."* |
| `It + is + adj + that-clause` | *"**It** is understood **that Mr. Gann will resign**."* |
| `It + was + noun + that` (emphasis) | *"**It** was **Jane** that gave a speech."* |
| `It + is + prep phrase + that` (emphasis) | *"**It** is **in the conference room** that meetings take place."* |

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — Only `it` Works as Dummy Subject

> `that` and `there` **cannot** replace `it` in this role.

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Reason |
|---------|---------|--------|
| *"**That** was a cat that was stuck in the tree."* | *"**It** was a cat..."* | `That` = reference/demonstrative, not dummy |
| *"**There** is possible that nobody will attend."* | *"**It** is possible that..."* | `There` = existence only |
| *"**What** is always best to choose..."* | *"**It** is always best to choose..."* | `What` = interrogative, not dummy |

---

## 📋 `it` vs. `there` — Quick Decision

| Use `It` when... | Use `There` when... |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Sentence expresses an **opinion/idea**: *"It is a good idea to..."* | Sentence expresses **existence**: *"There is a guidebook..."* |
| Followed by adjective, noun, or prepositional phrase | Followed by a noun phrase (real subject) |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `that` instead of `it`:** `that` cannot be a dummy subject. If you see `that is + adjective + to...` ❌ → `it is + adjective + to...` ✅
- **Trap 2 — `there` instead of `it`:** `there` signals existence. `it` signals opinion/fact. `"There is possible that..."` ❌ → `"It is possible that..."` ✅
- **Trap 3 — Wrong verb form after `It is adj + to`:** `It is mandatory to **obtaining**` ❌ → `to **obtain**` ✅ (bare infinitive after `to`)
- **Trap 4 — `what` as dummy subject:** `what` = relative pronoun, never dummy subject

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Is the blank at the start of the sentence, followed by `is/was + adjective/noun + to/that`?
> → The answer is `it` (dummy subject).
>
> **Step 2:** Is the sentence expressing an **opinion or fact** (important, surprising, clear)?
> → `it` ✅ (not `there`)
>
> **Step 3:** Is the sentence expressing **existence** (there are, there remain)?
> → `there` ✅ (not `it`)
>
> **Step 4:** After `It is adj + to` → bare infinitive follows `to`.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"When deciding what food to serve guests at a party, _____ is always best to choose dishes that can be prepared ahead of time."*

- (A) there · (B) what · (C) that · **(D) it ✅**

> 💡 `_____ is always best to choose...` → opinion structure + to-infinitive → **dummy subject `it`**. `there` = existence; `that/what` cannot be dummy subjects.

### Q2
> *"It is mandatory to _____ a work identification card when you first enter the company."*

- **(A) obtain ✅** · (B) obtaining · (C) obtainment · (D) obtained

> 💡 `It is mandatory to _____` → `to` + bare infinitive → `obtain`. `obtaining` = gerund ❌; `obtainment` = noun ❌; `obtained` = past form ❌.

### Q3
> *"Although the two copywriters are often at odds, _____ is clear that they have respect for each other's work."*

- (A) there · **(B) it ✅** · (C) what · (D) that

> 💡 `_____ is clear that...` → expresses a **fact/opinion** + that-clause → **dummy subject `it`**. `there is clear` ❌ — `there` only introduces existence + noun phrase.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `that is possible that` | `it is possible that` | `that` = reference; `it` = dummy |
| `there is important to` | `it is important to` | `there` ≠ opinion; `it` = opinion/fact |
| `it is adj + to verb-ing` | `it is adj + to + bare verb` | After `to` → always bare infinitive |
| `what is necessary to` | `it is necessary to` | `what` = question/relative, never dummy |
