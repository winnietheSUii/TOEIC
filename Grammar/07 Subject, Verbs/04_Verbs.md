# 📌 Verbs — Conjugated Verbs & What Cannot Be a Verb
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 08 — Subjects & Verbs | Part 4 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Only Conjugated Verbs Can Be Main Verbs*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Every sentence must have a **conjugated verb** (a verb that is inflected/matched to its subject).
> **Verbals** (`-ing` forms and `to + verb`) look like verbs but cannot function as main verbs.
> **Nouns** and **adjectives** can never fill the verb slot either.

---

## 📋 Conjugated vs. Non-Conjugated Forms

| Form | Conjugated? | Can be main verb? | Example |
|------|-------------|-------------------|---------|
| `oversees` | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | *"The supervisor **oversees** an office."* |
| `can oversee` | ✅ Yes (with modal) | ✅ Yes | *"The supervisor **can oversee** an office."* |
| `overseeing` | ❌ No (verbal) | ❌ No | *"The supervisor **overseeing** an office."* ❌ |
| `to oversee` | ❌ No (verbal) | ❌ No | *"The supervisor **to oversee** an office."* ❌ |
| `oversight` | ❌ No (noun) | ❌ No | *"The supervisor **oversight** the office."* ❌ |
| `compliant` | ❌ No (adjective) | ❌ No | *"This company **compliant** with regulations."* ❌ |

---

## ⚠️ Two Illegal Verb Substitutions

### ❌ Verbals Cannot Be Main Verbs

> **Verbals** = `V-ing` (gerund/present participle) and `to + V` (to-infinitive)
> They come from verbs but **do not agree with the subject** — so they cannot be the main verb.

| Wrong | Right | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| *"The supervisor **overseeing** an entire office."* | *"The supervisor **oversees** an entire office."* | Add conjugation |
| *"The supervisor **to oversee** an entire office."* | *"The supervisor **oversees** an entire office."* | Add conjugation |

---

### ❌ Nouns and Adjectives Cannot Be Main Verbs

| Wrong | Right | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| *"Jake **arrival** a few minutes late."* | *"Jake **arrived** a few minutes late."* | `arrival` (noun) → `arrived` (verb) |
| *"This company **compliant** with regulations."* | *"This company **complies** with regulations."* | `compliant` (adjective) → `complies` (verb) |

---

## 🔑 Key Signal — Sentence Has Subject But No Verb

> If you see `Subject + [blank] + object/complement` and the blank has **no conjugated verb yet**, the blank needs a **conjugated main verb**.

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `-ing` form as main verb:** *"The team **organizing** the event."* ❌ → `organizes/organized` ✅
- **Trap 2 — `to + verb` as main verb:** *"The manager **to announce** the results."* ❌ → `announced` ✅
- **Trap 3 — Noun in verb slot:** *"Jake **arrival** at 9 a.m."* ❌ → `arrived` ✅ (`arrival` = noun)
- **Trap 4 — Adjective in verb slot:** *"They **compliant** with the policy."* ❌ → `comply` ✅

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the subject of the sentence.
> **Step 2:** Does the sentence have a conjugated verb yet? If **NO** → the blank is the **main verb** slot.
> **Step 3:** The blank must be a **conjugated verb** — inflected to match the subject.
> **Step 4:** Eliminate:
> - `-ing` forms (unless with `is/are` for progressive)
> - `to + verb` (unless part of a complement, not main verb)
> - Nouns (e.g., `arrival/compliance`)
> - Adjectives (e.g., `compliant/available`)

---

## 📝 Key Error Reference

| Wrong Form in Verb Slot | Correct Conjugated Verb |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| `overseeing` | `oversees / oversaw` |
| `to oversee` | `oversees / oversaw` |
| `arrival` | `arrived` |
| `compliant` | `complies / complied` |
| `the suggestion` | `suggests / suggested` |
| `available` | *(cannot become a verb — wrong word class)* |

---

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| `-ing` as main verb | Add `is/are` or change to conjugated form | `-ing` alone = never main verb |
| `to + verb` as main verb | Remove `to`, add correct tense | `to-infinitive` = complement, not main verb |
| Noun in verb slot | Convert to verb | `arrival` → `arrived`; `decision` → `decided` |
| Adjective in verb slot | Convert to verb | `compliant` → `complies`; `available` → *(not directly a verb)* |

> ✏️ **Quick Test:**
> Does the word in the blank **agree with a subject** (change for 3rd person/past)?
> If YES → it's conjugated. If NO → it's a verbal or non-verb form. Keep looking.
