# 📌 Number, Tense & Voice of a Verb
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 08 — Subjects & Verbs | Part 5 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Three Agreement Rules Every Verb Must Follow*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Every verb in a sentence must be correct in three dimensions:
> 1. **Number** — matches singular or plural subject
> 2. **Tense** — matches the time expression in the sentence
> 3. **Voice** — active or passive based on who performs the action

---

## 📂 Rule 1 — Number Agreement

> Subject and verb must **match in number**.
> Singular subject → singular verb | Plural subject → plural verb

### ❌ Errors

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| *"The **results** of the inquiry **was** made public."* | *"...were made public."* | `results` = plural → `were` |
| *"The **prospect** of increased revenue **await** us."* | *"...awaits us."* | `prospect` = singular → `awaits` |

> ⚠️ **Distractor Warning:** Words between subject and verb (e.g., `of the inquiry`) do **not** change the verb. Focus on the **head noun** of the subject.

---

## 📂 Rule 2 — Tense Agreement

> The verb tense must match the **time expression** (signal words) in the sentence.

| Time Expression | Required Tense | Example |
|----------------|---------------|---------|
| `seven years ago` / `last year` / `in 2015` | **Past Simple** | *"Randy **joined** the firm seven years ago."* |
| `currently` / `now` / `at the moment` | **Present Progressive** | *"She is **currently working** on the project."* |
| `already` / `since` / `for` + duration | **Present Perfect** | *"He has **worked** here since 2018."* |
| `tomorrow` / `next year` / `soon` | **Future** | *"The report **will be** published next month."* |

### ❌ Error

| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| *"Randy **joins** the firm seven years ago."* | *"Randy **joined** the firm seven years ago."* |

---

## 📂 Rule 3 — Voice Agreement (Active vs. Passive)

> **Active voice:** Subject performs the action → `Subject + verb + object`
> **Passive voice:** Subject receives the action → `Subject + be + past participle (+ by...)`

| Subject Type | Voice | Example |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| Subject **performs** action | Active | *"They **used** their savings for a charitable cause."* |
| Subject **receives** action | Passive | *"The accounting reports **were checked** by a team of auditors."* |

### ❌ Errors

| Wrong | Right | Problem |
|-------|-------|---------|
| *"The accounting reports **checked** by a team of auditors."* | *"...were checked..."* | Missing `were` — passive needs `be + p.p.` |
| *"They **were used** their savings for a charitable cause."* | *"They **used** their savings..."* | `They` = active agent → active voice |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Distractor between subject and verb:** *"The **success** of our efforts **depend** on teamwork."* ❌ → `success` = singular → `depends` ✅
- **Trap 2 — Time expression ignored:** `seven years ago` → must be past simple, not present
- **Trap 3 — Missing `be` in passive:** `"The report checked by..."` ❌ → `"was checked by..."` ✅
- **Trap 4 — Passive with active agent subject:** `"They were used their savings"` ❌ → `"They used their savings"` ✅ (they = doer = active)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **For Number:** Find the main subject → count it (singular/plural) → match verb form.
> **For Tense:** Scan for time expressions → match tense to signal word.
> **For Voice:** Is the subject the **doer** or **receiver** of the action?
> - Doer → active (`verb`)
> - Receiver → passive (`be + past participle`)

---

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1 — Number
> *"The results of the inquiry _____ made public today."*
- `were` ✅ — `results` = plural head noun → `were`

### Q2 — Tense
> *"Randy _____ the firm seven years ago."*
- `joined` ✅ — `seven years ago` = past time expression

### Q3 — Voice
> *"The accounting reports _____ by a team of auditors."*
- `were checked` ✅ — `reports` = receiver, not doer → passive = `were checked`

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Rule | Mistake | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| **Number** | Matching verb to distractor noun | Focus on **head noun** of subject only |
| **Tense** | Ignoring time signal words | Always scan for `ago/since/now/tomorrow` first |
| **Voice** | Active subject with passive verb | Ask: "Does the subject **do** or **receive** the action?" |
| **Passive** | Forgetting `be` | Passive = `be + past participle` — `be` is never optional |
