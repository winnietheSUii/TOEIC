# 📌 Verbs in the Imperative
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 08 — Subjects & Verbs | Part 6 of 6**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Imperative = Bare Infinitive · Common Lead-In Structures*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> An **imperative sentence** gives a command, instruction, or request.
> The main verb in an imperative clause must always be a **bare infinitive** (base form, no `to`).
> The implied subject is always `you` (not stated).
> TOEIC tests this in the context of lead-in clauses: `When...`, `If...`, and `Whatever...`.

---

## 📐 Core Pattern

```
[Optional: When/If/Whatever clause,] + BARE INFINITIVE + ...
```

| Example | Structure |
|---------|-----------|
| *"**Place** the report on my desk."* | Bare infinitive starts the sentence |
| *"Please **complete** this form and sign at the bottom."* | `Please` + bare infinitive |
| *"When investigating an insurance claim, **follow** all company procedures."* | `When + participial phrase` + bare infinitive |
| *"If you would like a transfer, **submit** this form."* | `If-clause` + bare infinitive |
| *"Whatever your destination, **rely** on J Net for quality service."* | `Whatever + noun` + bare infinitive |

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — Only Bare Infinitive in Imperative Slot

> Conjugated verbs, verbals, and nouns cannot fill this slot.

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Problem |
|---------|---------|---------|
| *"Please **has** a seat."* | *"Please **have** a seat."* | `has` = conjugated (3rd person) |
| *"Whatever your destination, **relying** on J Net..."* | *"...rely on J Net..."* | `relying` = gerund |
| *"When negotiating a partnership, **trial** to be open-minded."* | *"...try to be open-minded."* | `trial` = noun |

---

## 📋 Lead-In Structures Before the Imperative

| Lead-In | Example |
|---------|---------|
| `When + V-ing phrase,` | *"When **negotiating** a contract, **seek** legal advice."* |
| `If + clause,` | *"If the strategy fails, **evaluate** the plan."* |
| `Whatever + noun phrase,` | *"Whatever your investment needs, **count** on Farallon Services."* |

> 💡 The lead-in clause does not affect the imperative — the **main clause still starts with bare infinitive**.

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — 3rd person `-s` in imperative:** `"avoids discussing matters"` ❌ → `avoid` ✅ (imperative always bare form)
- **Trap 2 — Gerund in imperative slot:** `"relying on J Net"` ❌ → `rely` ✅
- **Trap 3 — Noun in imperative slot:** `"trial to be open-minded"` ❌ → `try` ✅
- **Trap 4 — `to + verb` in imperative slot:** `"to evaluate the plan"` ❌ → `evaluate` ✅ (no `to` in imperative)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Does the sentence have a lead-in clause (`When...` / `If...` / `Whatever...`) followed by a comma, then a blank?
> → The blank **must be a bare infinitive**.
>
> **Step 2:** Does the sentence start with `Please` then a blank?
> → Bare infinitive.
>
> **Step 3:** Eliminate immediately:
> - `-s` forms (`avoids`, `counts`, `evaluates`)
> - `-ing` forms (`avoiding`, `counting`, `relying`)
> - `to + verb` (`to evaluate`, `to avoid`)
> - Nouns (`avoidance`, `evaluation`)

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"When making a presentation, _____ discussing matters already known to the audience."*

- (A) avoids · **(B) avoid ✅** · (C) avoiding · (D) avoidance

> 💡 `When + participial phrase, _____` → imperative structure → **bare infinitive** → `avoid`. `avoids` = 3rd person conjugated ❌; `avoiding` = gerund ❌; `avoidance` = noun ❌.

### Q2
> *"Whatever your investment needs, _____ on Farallon Services to provide the information and advice you need."*

- (A) counts · **(B) count ✅** · (C) counted · (D) counting

> 💡 `Whatever + noun phrase, _____` → imperative → bare infinitive → `count`. `counts` = 3rd person ❌; `counted` = past ❌; `counting` = gerund ❌.

### Q3
> *"If the strategy fails to meet the team's objectives, _____ the plan in order to determine where its weaknesses are."*

- (A) evaluating · (B) to evaluate · **(C) evaluate ✅** · (D) evaluation

> 💡 `If-clause, _____` → imperative → bare infinitive → `evaluate`. `evaluating` = gerund ❌; `to evaluate` = to-infinitive ❌; `evaluation` = noun ❌.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Trigger | Required Form | Example |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| `Please + _____` | Bare infinitive | `Please **submit** the form` |
| `When + V-ing, _____` | Bare infinitive | `When negotiating, **seek** advice` |
| `If + clause, _____` | Bare infinitive | `If it fails, **evaluate** the plan` |
| `Whatever + noun, _____` | Bare infinitive | `Whatever your need, **contact** us` |

> ✏️ **One Rule for All Imperatives:**
> No matter what comes before the comma — `When` / `If` / `Whatever` / `Please` —
> the imperative verb is **ALWAYS the bare infinitive**. No `-s`, no `-ing`, no `to`.
