# 📌 Expressions with Be + Adjective
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 03 — Adjectives | Part 4 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Fixed Adjective Collocations*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Many adjectives in English are paired with **fixed prepositions or verb forms**.
> These `be + adjective + preposition/infinitive` structures appear frequently in TOEIC Part 5.
> You must **memorize** these as complete chunks — not individual words.

---

## 📋 Essential `Be + Adjective` Expressions

| Expression | Structure | Example |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| **be about to** | be about to + V | *"The company is **about to** go bankrupt."* |
| **be apt to** | be apt to + V | *"Sales are **apt to** increase this season."* |
| **be likely to** | be likely to + V | *"The market is **likely to** drop by 2%."* |
| **be liable to** | be liable to + sth / to V | *"The firm is **liable to** face penalties."* |
| **be available to** | be available to + V | *"She was **available to** start immediately."* |
| **be available for** | be available for + noun | *"She is **available for** private consultation."* |
| **be aware of** | be aware of + noun | *"Everyone should **be aware of** the dangers."* |
| **be conscious of** | be conscious of + noun | *"She is **conscious of** the risks."* |
| **be capable of** | be capable of + V-ing | *"The team is **capable of** handling the project."* |
| **be comparable to** | be comparable to + noun | *"This year's figures are **comparable to** last year's."* |
| **be consistent with** | be consistent with + noun | *"Quality must **be consistent with** demand."* |
| **be eligible for** | be eligible for + noun | *"Freelancers are not **eligible for** the pension."* |
| **be eligible to** | be eligible to + V | *"She is **eligible to** apply for the position."* |
| **be responsible for** | be responsible for + noun | *"The police are **responsible for** the accident."* |
| **be skilled in/at** | be skilled in/at + noun | *"People **skilled in** web design are in demand."* |
| **be subject to** | be subject to + noun | *"This presentation may **be subject to** revision."* |
| **be willing to** | be willing to + V | *"He **is willing to** do whatever it takes."* |

---

## 🔑 Pattern Categories

### 🔹 `be + adj + to + infinitive`
> `be about to V` · `be apt to V` · `be likely to V` · `be eligible to V` · `be willing to V` · `be available to V`

### 🔹 `be + adj + for + noun`
> `be eligible for sth` · `be available for sth` · `be responsible for sth`

### 🔹 `be + adj + of + noun`
> `be aware of sth` · `be conscious of sth` · `be capable of V-ing`

### 🔹 `be + adj + with + noun`
> `be consistent with sth` · `be comparable to sth` (note: `to` not `with`)

### 🔹 `be + adj + to + noun`
> `be subject to sth` · `be comparable to sth`

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — `apt` vs `about` vs `likely`:** All can precede `to + V`, but meaning differs:
  - `be about to` = imminent (happening very soon)
  - `be apt to` / `be likely to` = probable/expected
- **Trap 2 — `subject` vs `subjective`:** `be subject to` = will undergo/be affected by. `subjective` = based on personal opinion — completely different
- **Trap 3 — `eligible` form:** `eligibility` = noun · `eligible` = adjective · `eligibly` = adverb. The expression needs the **adjective** form → `be eligible`
- **Trap 4 — `capable of + V-ing`:** After `capable of` → always **gerund** (`-ing`), not infinitive

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Identify the structure: `be + _____ + preposition/to`
> **Step 2:** Look at what comes **after** the blank:
> - `to + V` → likely adj options: `apt`, `likely`, `about`, `willing`, `eligible`, `available`
> - `for + noun` → likely: `eligible`, `available`, `responsible`
> - `of + noun/V-ing` → likely: `aware`, `conscious`, `capable`
> - `to + noun` → likely: `subject`, `comparable`
> **Step 3:** Match the meaning of the sentence to choose between similar options.
> **Step 4:** Watch for wrong word forms — question will often include the noun or adverb form of the same word.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Given the recent trends in stocks and bonds, the market is _____ to drop by 2 percent in the next couple of quarters."*

- (A) apt · (B) about · **(C) likely ✅** · (D) capable

> 💡 All three (`apt`, `about`, `likely`) can precede `to + V`. Context: a prediction based on trends → `likely to` = probable. `about to` = happening immediately (imminent) — too certain for a prediction. `capable of` needs `of + V-ing`, not `to V`.

### Q2
> *"...application for membership...may be _____ to rejection."*

- (A) subjective · (B) subjecting · **(C) subject ✅** · (D) subjects

> 💡 `be subject to + noun` = fixed expression meaning "may undergo/be affected by." `subjective` = based on opinion — wrong meaning entirely. `subjecting` = present participle (verb form).

### Q3
> *"Persons with disabilities are not _____ to apply for labor positions because of the extensive physical demands."*

- (A) eligibility · (B) eligibleness · **(C) eligible ✅** · (D) eligibly

> 💡 `be eligible to + V` = fixed expression → needs **adjective** form. `eligibility` = noun. `eligibleness` = not a standard word. `eligibly` = adverb (cannot follow `be`).

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Expression | Trap Version | Correct |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| `be subject to` | `be subjective to` | `subject` = affected by; `subjective` = personal opinion |
| `be eligible for/to` | `eligibility for` | Need adjective, not noun after `be` |
| `be capable of V-ing` | `be capable to V` | `of` → always followed by **gerund** |
| `be likely to V` vs `be about to V` | Probability vs. imminence | `likely` = expected; `about to` = happening right now |
| `be responsible for` | `be responsible of` | Always `for` — not `of` |

> ✏️ **Memorize as Chunks — not separate words:**
> → `be subject to` · `be eligible for` · `be aware of` · `be capable of` · `be consistent with`
> These are **fixed collocations** — learn the full expression, not just the adjective.
