# 📌 Positions of Adjectives
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 03 — Adjectives | Part 1 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Where Adjectives Go & What They Can't Do*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Adjectives **describe nouns**. They appear in two positions.
> TOEIC tests whether you can identify an adjective slot and avoid placing a noun or adverb there instead.

---

## 📍 Position 1 — Pre-nominal (Before the Noun)

> **Pattern:** `Article + (Adverb) + Adjective + Noun`

| Example | Structure |
|---------|-----------|
| *"You will get the **specific** information about the meeting."* | Article + adj + noun |
| *"The **highly successful** fair was attended by thousands."* | Adverb + adj + noun |
| *"John was awarded for his **exceptional** job performance."* | Adj + compound noun |

> Adjectives can modify **compound nouns** directly:
> *"exceptional **job performance**"* ✅

---

## 📍 Position 2 — Post-nominal / Predicative (After the Noun)

> Adjectives can appear as a **complement** — after a linking verb or after the noun in certain structures.

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Subject complement** (after `be`/linking verb) | *"It is **important** to be creative in advertising."* |
| **Object complement** (after verb + object) | *"Light-colored walls make a room **bright**."* |
| **After measure quantifier** | *"The bank will take every measure **possible**..."* |

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — Only Adjectives Can Modify Nouns

> **Nouns** and **adverbs** cannot fill adjective positions.
> Only the **adjective form** is correct in these slots.

### ❌ Common Errors

| Wrong | Right | Reason |
|-------|-------|--------|
| *"an **improperly** diet"* | *"an **improper** diet"* | `improperly` = adverb; cannot modify noun `diet` |
| *"an **experience** floor manager"* | *"an **experienced** floor manager"* | `experience` = noun; need adjective form |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Adverb in adjective slot:** `quickly action` ❌ → `quick action` ✅ (adverb cannot modify noun)
- **Trap 2 — Noun in adjective slot:** `experience manager` ❌ → `experienced manager` ✅
- **Trap 3 — `-ly` form before noun:** Even if `-ly` = adverb in other contexts, it **cannot** modify a noun
- **Trap 4 — Participle as adjective:** `presented` / `presenting` = verb forms. But `present` = adjective meaning "current" (context matters)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Is the blank **before a noun** or **after a linking verb** (is/are/seem/become)?
> → Both positions need an **adjective**.
>
> **Step 2:** Scan the options — eliminate:
> - Adverbs (`-ly` forms)
> - Nouns (`-tion`, `-ment`, `-ness`, `-er`)
> - Pure verb forms (`quicken`, `quickened`)
>
> **Step 3:** From remaining adjective options → choose by meaning.

---

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Commercial banks are advised to take _____ action to amend the serious currency shortfall."*

- (A) quickness · (B) quicken · (C) quickly · **(D) quick ✅**

> 💡 Before noun `action` → **adjective** → `quick`. `quickly` = adverb (cannot modify noun). `quickness` = noun. `quicken` = verb.

### Q2
> *"If we continue with the _____ production rate, inventory should be stocked by next week."*

- **(A) present ✅** · (B) presented · (C) presently · (D) presenting

> 💡 Before compound noun `production rate` → **adjective** → `present` (= current). `presently` = adverb. `presented/presenting` = verb forms.

### Q3
> *"Workers are much less _____ to detail during the day if they haven't had sufficient rest."*

- **(A) attentive ✅** · (B) attention · (C) attentively · (D) attentiveness

> 💡 After `be` verb (`are`) → **subject complement** → adjective. `be attentive to` = fixed expression. `attention` and `attentiveness` = nouns. `attentively` = adverb.

---

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---------|-----|--------------|
| Adverb before noun | Use adjective | *Only adjectives describe nouns* — adverbs describe verbs |
| Noun before noun | Use adjective form | `experience` → `experienced` (add `-d/-ed` for adj) |
| `-ing` / `-ed` verb form chosen | Check: is it modifying a noun? | Participial adjectives are valid, but check meaning |
| `presently` vs `present` | `present` = adjective (current) | Adverb slot ≠ before noun slot |

> ✏️ **Quick Check:**
> - Blank before **noun** → Adjective
> - Blank after **be/seem/become** → Adjective (subject complement)
> - Blank before **adjective or verb** → Adverb
