## 🧭 Overview

> This note consolidates all tense rules into a **mixed drill** format.
> Two drill types:
> 1. **Choose the right tense** for a given sentence.
> 2. **Choose the right signal word** to match a given tense.
> Use this as your pre-exam rapid-fire self-test.

---

## 📋 Master Signal Word → Tense Reference

| Signal Word | Tense |
|-------------|-------|
| `yesterday / last [week/year] / ago / in [past year]` | **Past Simple** |
| `usually / often / each [month] / always / normally` | **Present Simple** |
| `since [point] / for [duration] / already / just / recently / never / ever / this year` | **Present Perfect** |
| `now / at the moment / currently / right now / today` | **Present Progressive** |
| `at [past time] / while / when [interrupted]` | **Past Progressive** |
| `tomorrow / next [week/year] / soon / by [future date]` | **Future Simple** |
| `by [future time] / by the time [future event] / next [month] + duration` | **Future Perfect** |
| `at this time tomorrow / this time next week` | **Future Progressive** |

---

## ⚠️ Special Rules — Quick Reminders

| Rule | Summary |
|------|---------|
| **Stative verbs** | Never progressive — always simple (`need/include/prefer/believe`) |
| **Sequence of tenses** | Main clause past → sub-clause shifts to past |
| **Past reported future** | `will` becomes `would` in past reported speech |
| **`as soon as / when / if / before / after`** | Use present simple (not `will`) for future meaning |
| **`since`** | Always triggers present perfect |
| **`by the time` (future)** | Always triggers future perfect |

## 🔍 Error Identification Drill

| Wrong Sentence | Error | Corrected |
|----------------|-------|-----------|
| *"When Martin **reads** the contract, he discovered he had been deceived."* | `reads` → should be past | *"When Martin **read** the contract..."* |
| *"William reluctantly **attends** the seminar, but he surprisingly found it helpful."* | `attends` → should be past | *"...reluctantly **attended**..."* |
| *"After returning from vacation, we learned that a storm **damages** our house."* | `damages` → should be past perfect (earlier event) | *"...storm **had damaged**..."* |
| *"James **retire** years ago, but he still has stock in his former employer."* | `retire` → past signal `years ago` | *"James **retired** years ago..."* |
| *"The reports estimate that earnings **has doubled** by the end of next year."* | `has doubled` → future signal | *"...earnings **will double**..."* |
| *"If you **had believed** there may be financial wrongdoing, please notify your supervisor."* | `had believed` → general condition, not counterfactual | *"If you **believe**..."* |
| *"What the new IT specialist knows **was** useful when the office updates its programs."* | `was` → future reference | *"...knows **will be** useful..."* |
| *"As soon as the board **will make** its decision, the managers will implement the plan."* | `will make` → after `as soon as` → present simple | *"As soon as the board **makes**..."* |
| *"The Pioneer Fund **is** the company's biggest earner over the past four years."* | `is` → `over the past 4 years` = present perfect | *"The Pioneer Fund **has been**..."* |
| *"The company **is needing** to increase production capacity."* | `is needing` → stative | *"The company **needs**..."* |

## 🔍 Signal Word Matching Drill

| Given Tense | Correct Signal | Incorrect Signal |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| `will have served` (future perfect) | `by the time he retires` ✅ | `since` ❌ |
| `has analyzed` (present perfect) | `since 2000` ✅ | `in 2000` ❌ |
| `is a manager` (present) | `at the moment` ✅ | `two years ago` ❌ |
| `requires` (present simple – regular) | `usually` ✅ | `once` ❌ |

---

## 🧠 The 4-Step Tense Decision Process

```
Step 1 → Find ALL time signals in the sentence

Step 2 → Match signal to tense (use master table above)

Step 3 → Check for stative verb → if yes, use SIMPLE tense

Step 4 → Check for main/sub clause relationship:
          - Main = past → shift sub-clause to past
          - Main = past + future sub = would (not will)
          - After as soon as/when = present simple (not will)
```

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"When Martin read the contract, he _____ that he had been deceived."*

- (A) discover · (B) discovers · **(C) discovered ✅** · (D) will discover

> 💡 `read` (past simple) in the main clause. Both actions happened at the same time in the past → past simple → `discovered`.

### Q2
> *"After returning from vacation, we learned that a storm _____ our house."*

- (A) damages · (B) damaged · **(C) had damaged ✅** · (D) will damage

> 💡 `learned` = past simple (later event). Storm damage happened **before** we returned → earlier → **past perfect** → `had damaged`.

### Q3
> *"Mr. Hyland will have served as CEO of the company for ten years _____ he retires."*

- (A) since · **(B) by the time ✅** · (C) when · (D) until

> 💡 `will have served` = future perfect → needs a signal that points to a **future completion deadline** → `by the time` ✅. `since` = present perfect signal ❌.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Pattern | Tense Error | Correct Rule |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| `[verb] + years ago` | Present or perfect | → Past simple |
| `since [point]` + past simple | Past simple | → Present perfect |
| `is/are + stative verb + -ing` | Progressive | → Simple form |
| `as soon as + will` | Future | → Present simple |
| Past main + `that + present sub` | Present | → Past simple in sub-clause |
| `by the time + future` | Present perfect | → Future perfect (`will have`) |

> ✏️ **Speed Drill — Cover the right column:**
> - `Yesterday, she ___ (go)` → `went`
> - `She ___ here since 2018` → `has been`
> - `By next December, he ___ here for 10 years` → `will have worked`
> - `As soon as she ___, we'll start` → `arrives`
> - `The company ___ a pool and gym` → `includes` (stative)
> - `He ___ that the report ___ completed last Tuesday` → `said / was`
