# 📌 Present_Past_Future Simple
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 10 — Tenses | Part 1 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Progressive Tenses · Signal Words · Stative Verbs*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> This file covers the **three progressive tenses** and a critical rule:
> **stative verbs** (state/feeling verbs) can **never** be used in progressive form.
> TOEIC frequently swaps a progressive for a simple tense to test this distinction.

---

## 📂 The Three Progressive Tenses

### 1️⃣ Present Progressive — `am/is/are + V-ing`
> Used when an action is **happening right now** or is in progress at the time of speaking.

> *"She **is living** in Canada at the moment."*
> → She's there now and will continue to be.

**Signal words:** `now` · `at the moment` · `currently` · `right now` · `today` · `this week`

---

### 2️⃣ Past Progressive — `was/were + V-ing`
> Used when an action was **in progress at a specific point in the past**.

> *"I **was watching** TV at eight o'clock yesterday."*
> → At 8:00 PM yesterday, the watching was already happening.

**Signal words:** `at [time] yesterday` · `at that time` · `while` · `when [past event interrupted]`

---

### 3️⃣ Future Progressive — `will be + V-ing`
> Used when an action **will be in progress at a specific future time**.

> *"At this time tomorrow, I **will be lying** on the beach."*
> → Tomorrow at this exact time, the lying will be ongoing.

**Signal words:** `at this time tomorrow` · `by [future time]` · `this time next week`

---

## ⚠️ Core Rule — Stative Verbs CANNOT Use Progressive

> **Stative verbs** express a **state, feeling, or mental condition** — not a physical action.
> They describe something that **simply is** — it doesn't happen or unfold over time.
> → **Never** use `-ing` with stative verbs.

### Stative Verb List (TOEIC High-Frequency)

| Category | Verbs |
|----------|-------|
| **Feelings/Preferences** | `like · love · hate · prefer · want · wish` |
| **Mental States** | `know · believe · think (opinion) · understand · remember · forget` |
| **Senses** | `see · hear · smell · taste · feel (judgment)` |
| **Possession** | `have · own · belong · possess · contain` |
| **Existence/State** | `be · exist · include · consist · seem · appear · need` |

### ❌ Error Examples

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| *"She **is preferring** the sound of the sea."* | *"She **prefers** the sound of the sea."* | `prefer` = stative |
| *"The president **is being** out of the office."* | *"The president **is** out of the office."* | `be` (state) = stative |
| *"Facilities **are including** a pool."* | *"Facilities **include** a pool."* | `include` = stative |
| *"She **is needing** more time."* | *"She **needs** more time."* | `need` = stative |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Progressive for stative verb:** `"are including"` ❌ → `include` ✅
- **Trap 2 — Wrong progressive tense:** Check signal word. `at 8 this morning` → past progressive (`was + V-ing`), not present or future
- **Trap 3 — Future progressive with `by the year`:** `"by the year 2020"` = specific future point → future progressive `will be + V-ing` ✅
- **Trap 4 — `is being` for permanent state:** `"is being out of the office"` ❌ → `is` ✅ (`be` for state = not progressive)

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the **signal word** in the sentence — what time does it point to?
> - Now/currently → present progressive
> - At [past time]/while/when → past progressive
> - At this time tomorrow/by [future time] → future progressive
>
> **Step 2:** Check whether the verb is **stative**:
> - Does it describe a feeling, opinion, possession, or existence? → **Simple tense** (no `-ing`)
> - Does it describe an **action** in progress? → **Progressive** is valid
>
> **Step 3:** Match tense to signal word; eliminate stative-verb progressive forms.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The majority of home designers believe that more people in the United States _____ in townhouses or gated communities by the year 2020."*

- (A) had been lived · (B) lived · **(C) will be living ✅** · (D) is living

> 💡 `by the year 2020` = specific future time → action in progress at that future point → **future progressive** → `will be living`.

### Q2
> *"Facilities at the university recreational center _____ an Olympic-sized swimming pool, five racquetball courts, and an indoor track."*

- (A) are including · **(B) include ✅** · (C) have included · (D) included

> 💡 `include` = stative verb (describes what the facilities contain) → **no progressive** → `include` (present simple).

### Q3
> *"The company president _____ the applicant for the accounting manager position at 8 this morning."*

- (A) interview · (B) interviewed · **(C) was interviewing ✅** · (D) will interview

> 💡 `at 8 this morning` = specific past time point → action was in progress at that moment → **past progressive** → `was interviewing`.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Signal Word | Tense | Form |
|-------------|-------|------|
| `now / at the moment / currently` | Present Progressive | `is/are + V-ing` |
| `at [past time] / while / when` | Past Progressive | `was/were + V-ing` |
| `at this time tomorrow / by [future time]` | Future Progressive | `will be + V-ing` |
| Stative verb (include/prefer/need/know) | **Always Simple** | Never add `-ing` |

> ✏️ **Stative Quick Test:**
> Can you say *"I am [verb]-ing right now"* and it sounds natural for a physical action?
> - `"I am running"` ✅ → action verb → progressive OK
> - `"I am preferring"` ❌ → sounds wrong → stative → use simple
