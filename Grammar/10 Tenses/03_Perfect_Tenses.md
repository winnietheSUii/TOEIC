# 📌 Perfect Tenses
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 10 — Tenses | Part 3 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Tense-Signal Matching · Sequence of Tenses in Subordinate Clauses*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Two rules govern tense accuracy in complex sentences:
> 1. **Verb must match the time signal** in the sentence.
> 2. **Subordinate clauses must agree in tense** with the main clause.
> These are the two most tested "tense logic" patterns in TOEIC Part 5 & 6.

---

## 📂 Rule 1 — Verb Must Match the Time Expression

> The time signal word in the sentence tells you **exactly which tense to use**. There is no guessing.

| Time Signal | Required Tense | Example |
|-------------|---------------|---------|
| `yesterday / last week / ago / in [past year]` | Past Simple | *"She **started** work last year."* |
| `usually / often / every day / each month` | Present Simple | *"She **teaches** English every morning."* |
| `since / for + duration` | Present Perfect | *"She **has taught** English since she moved here."* |
| `recently / just / already` | Present Perfect | *"TV viewership **has dropped** recently."* |
| `tomorrow / next week / soon` | Future Simple | *"Earnings **will double** by the end of next year."* |
| `by [future time] / by the time + future` | Future Perfect | *"She **will have served** for 14 years by the time she retires."* |

### ❌ Error Examples

| Wrong | Signal Clue | Right |
|-------|------------|-------|
| *"She **has started** to work last year."* | `last year` = past → past simple | *"She **started** to work last year."* |
| *"She **taught** English since she moved here."* | `since` = present perfect | *"She **has taught** English since..."* |
| *"She **will serve** for 14 years by the time she retires."* | `by the time` = future perfect | *"She **will have served**..."* |

## 📂 Rule 2 — Sequence of Tenses in Subordinate Clauses

> When the **main clause verb is past simple**, the verb in the **subordinate/that-clause** must also shift to past.

| Main Clause | Subordinate Clause |
|------------|-------------------|
| Past simple | Past simple (same time) or Past perfect (earlier) |
| Present/Future | Any tense that fits the meaning |

### ❌ Error Example

> *"Mr. MacKenzie **explained** that the fiscal report **is completed** the preceding week."*
> ❌ `explained` = past simple → that-clause must shift → `was completed` ✅

---

## 📋 The `as soon as` Rule

> After `as soon as` / `when` / `if` / `before` / `after` / `once` in future sentences:
> → Use **present simple** (not future) in the subordinate clause.

> *"**As soon as** the board **makes** its decision, the managers will implement the plan."* ✅
> *"As soon as the board **will make**..."* ❌ → never future after these conjunctions

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Wrong tense for signal word:** `since` → present perfect (never past simple); `last week` → past simple (never present perfect)
- **Trap 2 — Main clause past + subordinate present:** `"explained that...is"` ❌ → `"explained that...was"` ✅
- **Trap 3 — Future in `as soon as` clause:** `"as soon as she will arrive"` ❌ → `"as soon as she arrives"` ✅
- **Trap 4 — `would` in past reported speech:** Main clause past → future in that-clause becomes `would` → `"was informed that it would arrive"` ✅

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find all **time signals** in the sentence — they directly tell you the tense.
> **Step 2:** Match verb to signal (use the table above).
> **Step 3:** If there's a **main clause + subordinate clause**, check the main verb tense:
> - Main = past → subordinate shifts to past too
> - Main = past → future meaning in sub-clause = `would` (not `will`)
> **Step 4:** After `as soon as / when / if` in future sentences → present simple (no `will`).

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Television viewership slightly _____ since the advent of the Information Age."*

- (A) drop · (B) will drop · **(C) has dropped ✅** · (D) dropping

> 💡 `since` = present perfect signal → `has dropped`. `drop` = present simple ❌; `will drop` = future ❌.

### Q2
> *"Last week, a newspaper columnist _____ an article that triggered an investigation."*

- (A) writes · (B) has written · **(C) wrote ✅** · (D) have written

> 💡 `Last week` = definite past → **past simple** → `wrote`. `has written` = present perfect ❌ (cannot go with `last week`).

### Q3
> *"Management was informed that the shipment from the branch office in Paris _____ the following Thursday."*

- (A) arriving · **(B) would arrive ✅** · (C) have arrived · (D) has arrived

> 💡 Main clause = `was informed` (past simple). The shipment arrival is a **future event reported from the past** → `would arrive` (past form of "will arrive"). `has arrived` = present perfect ❌.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Pattern | Rule | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| `[past main] + that + [present sub]` | Shift to past | `explained that it **was**` (not `is`) |
| `[past main] + that + [future sub]` | Use `would` | `informed that it **would** arrive` |
| `since` + past simple | Switch to perfect | `since` needs `have/has + p.p.` |
| `as soon as` + `will` | Remove `will` | `as soon as it **arrives**` (not `will arrive`) |
| `by the time` + simple | Future perfect | `by the time she retires` → `will have served` |
