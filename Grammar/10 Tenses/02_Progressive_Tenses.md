# 📌 Progressive Tenses
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 10 — Tenses | Part 2 of 4**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Present Perfect · Past Perfect · Future Perfect*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> The **perfect tenses** all emphasize **completion relative to a time reference**.
> Each one anchors an action to a different moment: present, past, or future.
> TOEIC tests these heavily through **signal word recognition**.

---

## 📂 The Three Perfect Tenses

### 1️⃣ Present Perfect — `have/has + past participle`

> Used when an action **started in the past and is still relevant now**, or when the exact time is unknown/unimportant.

**Four uses:**

| Use | Signal | Example |
|-----|--------|---------|
| Duration from past to now | `for / since` | *"He **has served** for 5 years on the board."* |
| Just completed | `already / just / recently` | *"I **have already finished** my project."* |
| Never done | `never / ever` | *"I **have never visited** Mexico."* |
| Past action with present result | (no specific time) | *"I **have lost** my watch."* (effect = still lost now) |

> ⚠️ **Never use present perfect with a definite past time** (`yesterday / last year / in 2010 / ago`).
> *"She **has started** work last year."* ❌ → `started` ✅

---

### 2️⃣ Past Perfect — `had + past participle`

> Used when **two actions both happened in the past**, and you need to show which one happened **first**.

> *"When the party **finished**, my sister **had already departed**."*
> → Departed (past perfect) = happened FIRST. Party finished (past simple) = happened SECOND.

**Signal words:** `when / after / before / already / by the time` (all referring to past)

> 💡 **Rule of thumb:** The action that happened **earlier** gets `had + p.p.`. The later action uses simple past.

---

### 3️⃣ Future Perfect — `will have + past participle`

> Used when an action **will be completed before a specific future point**.

> *"I **will have studied** for 5 years by next December."*
> → The studying will be done before/by next December.

**Signal words:** `by [future time]` · `by the time [future event]` · `by next [week/year]`

---

## 📋 Perfect Tense Signal Word Master Table

| Signal Word | Use With |
|-------------|---------|
| `since [past point]` | Present Perfect |
| `for [duration]` | Present Perfect |
| `already / just / recently` | Present Perfect |
| `never / ever` | Present Perfect |
| `this year / this month` | Present Perfect |
| `yesterday / last week / in [year] / ago` | ❌ NOT perfect → **Past Simple** |
| `after/before/when` (2 past actions) | Past Perfect (earlier action) |
| `by [future time]` | Future Perfect |
| `by the time [future event]` | Future Perfect |
| `next fall / next December` + duration | Future Perfect |

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Present perfect + definite past time:** `"has started last year"` ❌ → `started` ✅ (`last year` = specific past → past simple)
- **Trap 2 — Simple past instead of past perfect:** Two past events, earlier one needs `had + p.p.`
- **Trap 3 — `has been` vs `will have been`:** `"next fall"` = future reference → `will have been` ✅ (not `has been`)
- **Trap 4 — `since` triggers:** `"since 2000"` = present perfect — never past simple with `since`

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the **time signal** in the sentence.
> **Step 2:** Match it to the correct tense:
> - `since/for/already/never/this year` → **Present Perfect** (`have/has + p.p.`)
> - `yesterday/last year/ago/in [past year]` → **Past Simple**
> - Two past events, earlier one → **Past Perfect** (`had + p.p.`)
> - `by [future time] / next fall` → **Future Perfect** (`will have + p.p.`)
> **Step 3:** Check if sentence has a future reference — don't use `has been` where `will have been` is needed.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"This year the car industry _____ many investors because automotive products now make up 15 percent of the country's exports."*

- (A) attract · **(B) has attracted ✅** · (C) attracting · (D) had attracted

> 💡 `this year` + still relevant now → **present perfect** → `has attracted`.

### Q2
> *"By exercising at the gym during lunch time, she could relieve work-related stress that _____ built up from the morning."*

- (A) has · (B) would · (C) has been · **(D) had been ✅**

> 💡 Two past events: stress building up (earlier) → she exercised (later). Earlier action → **past perfect** → `had been`.

### Q3
> *"Next fall, Irene _____ employed with this corporation for over a decade as an advisor to the president."*

- (A) has been · (B) is · **(C) will have been ✅** · (D) will be

> 💡 `Next fall` = future reference point. By then, she will have completed a decade. → **Future perfect** → `will have been`.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Signal |
|---------|-----|--------|
| `has started last year` | `started` | `last year` = definite past → past simple |
| `has attracted` with past signal | Past simple | Never present perfect with specific past time |
| Two past events, both simple past | Earlier → `had + p.p.` | Before/after/when = sequence signal |
| `has been` for future action | `will have been` | `next fall / by 2030` → future perfect |
| `since 2000` + past simple | `since 2000` + present perfect | `since` always needs present perfect |
