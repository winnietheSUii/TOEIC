# 📌 Noun ↔ Verb Forms
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 05 — Word Forms | Part 2 of 5**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Nominalisation & Verb Recognition*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> TOEIC frequently offers both a **noun** and a **verb** derived from the same root as answer choices.
> The key is to identify whether the blank is in a **noun position** (subject/object) or a **verb position** (predicate).

---

## 🔑 How to Tell Noun Position from Verb Position

| Signal Before Blank | Required Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Article: `a / an / the` | **Noun** | `the ___` → noun |
| Possessive: `our / their / company's` | **Noun** | `their ___` → noun |
| Preposition: `of / in / for / to` | **Noun** | `for ___` → noun |
| Adjective before blank | **Noun** | `significant ___` → noun |
| Subject + gap | **Verb** | `The company ___ its report` → verb |
| Auxiliary before blank | **Verb** | `has ___ / will ___ / is ___` | verb |

## ⚠️ Common Error Patterns

### Error 1 — Verb in noun position

> ❌ *"The company's ability to **compete** in a global market is critical."*
> (Here `compete` is actually correct — infinitive after `ability to`)

> ❌ *"We need to discuss the **improve** of our sales strategy."*
> ✅ *"We need to discuss the **improvement** of our sales strategy."*
> → After `the` → noun required → `improvement`

---

### Error 2 — Noun in verb position

> ❌ *"The board will **evaluation** the proposals next week."*
> ✅ *"The board will **evaluate** the proposals next week."*
> → After modal `will` → verb required → `evaluate`

---

### Error 3 — Wrong noun form (person vs. process)

> ❌ *"We hired an **operation** to run the new facility."*
> ✅ *"We hired an **operator** to run the new facility."*
> → Need a **person** (someone to hire) → `-or` suffix = person

---

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — After `the`:** Always a noun. "the **delivery**" ✅ / "the **deliver**" ❌
- **Trap 2 — After a modal:** Always a base verb. "will **improve**" ✅ / "will **improvement**" ❌
- **Trap 3 — Gerund as subject:** `-ing` form can be a noun (subject). "**Managing** staff is difficult." ✅ — don't confuse with verb form
- **Trap 4 — `-tion` vs `-tor`:** Both are nouns, but one = process, one = person. "an **operation**" ≠ "an **operator**"

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** Find the blank — is it in **subject/object position** or **predicate position**?
> **Step 2:** Check signal words:
> - Article / possessive / preposition before blank → **Noun**
> - Auxiliary verb / subject already present, blank follows → **Verb**
> **Step 3:** Choose the correct form — if noun, check: do you need a person or a process?
> **Step 4:** Eliminate verbs for noun slots and nouns for verb slots immediately.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The HR department will _____ all job applications within two weeks."*

- (A) review ✅
- (B) reviewer
- (C) review  *(same word used as noun)*
- (D) reviewed

> 💡 `will _____` → auxiliary + verb slot → **base verb** → `review`. `reviewer` = noun (person); `reviewed` = past tense/participle.

---

### Q2
> *"The _____ of the new policy has been postponed until further notice."*

- (A) implement
- (B) implemented
- (C) implementation ✅
- (D) implementing

> 💡 `The _____` → article → **noun** slot. `implementation` = `-tion` noun. `implement` = verb form.

---

### Q3
> *"Customer _____ is the top priority for all customer-facing staff."*

- (A) satisfy
- (B) satisfying
- (C) satisfactory
- (D) satisfaction ✅

> 💡 `Customer _____` → compound noun structure → **noun** needed. `satisfaction` = `-tion` noun = the state of being satisfied.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---|---|---|
| Verb after `the / a / an` | Use noun form | Article = noun always follows |
| Noun after modal (`will/can/must`) | Use base verb | Modal + base verb (no `-tion`) |
| Process noun vs person noun | Read context (hire/serve/work as → person) | `-tor/-er/-ist` = person; `-tion/-ment` = process |
| `-ing` form (gerund) confusion | Gerund is a noun — but only as subject | "**Managing** staff" ✅ as subject |

> ✏️ **Quick Reminder:**
> - `a/the/our + ___` → **Noun** (the **improvement**)
> - `will/can/must + ___` → **Verb** (will **improve**)
> - `has/have + ___` → **Past Participle** (has **improved**)
> - `is/are + ___ + -ing` → **Verb (continuous)** (is **improving**)
