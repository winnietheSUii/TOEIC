# 📌 Adverb Forms
**TOEIC Grammar | Unit 05 — Word Forms | Part 4 of 5**
*Level: B1–B2 | Focus: Forming Adverbs, Flat Adverbs & Tricky -ly Words*

---

## 🧭 Overview

> Most adverbs in TOEIC are formed by adding **`-ly`** to an adjective.
> But some adverbs don't change form at all (flat adverbs), and some `-ly` words are actually adjectives — not adverbs.
> This note covers how to form, recognize, and use adverbs correctly.

---

## 📐 Forming Adverbs from Adjectives

| Adjective Ending | How to Form Adverb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Regular (consonant) | + `-ly` | `quick` → `quickly` |
| Ends in `-ic` | + `-ally` | `automatic` → `automatically` |
| Ends in `-le` | Drop `-e` + `-y` | `simple` → `simply` |
| Ends in `-y` | Change `-y` to `-i` + `-ly` | `easy` → `easily` |
| Ends in `-ue` | Drop `-e` + `-ly` | `true` → `truly` |
| Ends in `-ll` | + `-y` | `full` → `fully` |

## 🔄 Flat Adverbs — Same Form as Adjective

> These words function as **both adjective and adverb** — no `-ly` needed.

| Word | As Adjective | As Adverb |
|---|---|---|
| `hard` | a **hard** worker | work **hard** |
| `fast` | a **fast** car | drive **fast** |
| `high` | a **high** salary | climb **high** |
| `long` | a **long** meeting | wait **long** |
| `late` | a **late** submission | arrive **late** |
| `early` | an **early** deadline | leave **early** |
| `far` | a **far** distance | travel **far** |
| `near` | a **near** solution | come **near** |
| `daily` | a **daily** report | meet **daily** |
| `weekly` | a **weekly** review | update **weekly** |
| `monthly` | a **monthly** fee | meet **monthly** |
| `yearly` | a **yearly** bonus | increase **yearly** |
| `straight` | a **straight** path | go **straight** |

> ⚠️ Adding `-ly` to some flat adverbs **changes the meaning**:
> - `hard` (with effort) vs `hardly` (almost not)
> - `high` (physical height) vs `highly` (very much / figuratively)
> - `late` (not on time) vs `lately` (recently)
> - `near` (physical closeness) vs `nearly` (almost)

## ⚠️ Tricky `-ly` Words That Are Actually ADJECTIVES

> These look like adverbs because of `-ly`, but they are **adjectives** — they modify nouns!

| Word | Class | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `friendly` | Adjective | a **friendly** atmosphere |
| `timely` | Adjective | a **timely** response |
| `costly` | Adjective | a **costly** mistake |
| `lively` | Adjective | a **lively** discussion |
| `lonely` | Adjective | a **lonely** position |
| `likely` | Adjective | a **likely** outcome |
| `quarterly` | Adjective/Adverb | **quarterly** reports / paid **quarterly** |
| `monthly` | Adjective/Adverb | **monthly** salary / billed **monthly** |
| `weekly` | Adjective/Adverb | **weekly** meeting / updated **weekly** |
| `orderly` | Adjective | an **orderly** process |
| `scholarly` | Adjective | a **scholarly** article |

## 🎯 Common TOEIC Traps

- **Trap 1 — Choosing adjective for adverb slot:** "The project was completed **successful**" ❌ → "**successfully**" ✅
- **Trap 2 — Choosing `hardly` when context means effort:** "She works **hardly**" ❌ (= she barely works) → "She works **hard**" ✅
- **Trap 3 — `-ly` word treated as adverb:** "The company responded **timely**" ❌ — `timely` is an adjective, not an adverb. Say: "The company gave a **timely** response."
- **Trap 4 — `automatically` vs `automatic`:** Before a noun → `automatic response` (adj). Modifying a verb → "processed **automatically**" (adv).

---

## 🧠 Exam Thinking Strategy

> **Step 1:** What does the blank modify?
> - A **verb, adjective, or another adverb** → use **adverb** (`-ly` or flat)
> - A **noun** → use **adjective** (NOT `-ly`)
>
> **Step 2:** Is the base adjective ending in `-ic`?
> → Add `-ally` (not just `-ly`): `systematic` → `systematically` ✅ (not `systematicly` ❌)
>
> **Step 3:** Is the word a flat adverb (hard, fast, late...)?
> → No `-ly` needed. But check: does adding `-ly` change the meaning?
>
> **Step 4:** If the options include a `-ly` word you're unsure about (e.g., `timely`):
> → Ask: "Can it come before a noun?" If yes → it's an adjective form.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"Please respond to the customer inquiry as _____ as possible."*

- (A) quick
- (B) quickly ✅
- (C) quickness
- (D) quicken

> 💡 "as _____ as possible" = adverb in an `as...as` equality structure → `quickly` ✅. (`as quick as` is informal; TOEIC prefers the adverb form.)

---

### Q2
> *"The new system processes all orders _____, eliminating manual errors."*

- (A) automatic
- (B) automation
- (C) automatically ✅
- (D) automate

> 💡 Blank modifies the verb `processes` → **adverb** → `automatically` (`-ic` base → `-ically`). `automatic` = adjective (would need to be before a noun).

---

### Q3
> *"The manager gave a _____ response to the client's urgent request."*

- (A) timely ✅
- (B) timelessly
- (C) time
- (D) timing

> 💡 Before noun `response` → **adjective** needed. `timely` = an `-ly` adjective (done at the right time). `timelessly` = adverb, cannot modify a noun.

## 🗂️ Error Log / Memory Tips

| Mistake | Fix | Memory Trick |
|---|---|---|
| `successful` after a verb | `successfully` | Verb needs adverb not adjective |
| `hardly` when effort is meant | `hard` | `hardly` = almost not (negative!) |
| `-ic` → `-icly` | `-ic` → `-ically` | `automatic` → `automatically` (not `automaticly`) |
| `timely` used as adverb | Use as adjective before noun | "a **timely** response" — timely cannot be an adverb |
| Flat adverb + `-ly` = meaning change | Memorize the 6 pairs | hard/hardly, high/highly, late/lately, near/nearly |

> ✏️ **Adverb Formation Shortcuts:**
> - Regular adj → + `-ly` → adverb
> - Ends `-ic` → + `-ally` → adverb
> - Ends `-le` → `-ly` (drop e) → adverb
> - Ends `-y` → `-ily` → adverb
> - Flat adverbs → no change needed
