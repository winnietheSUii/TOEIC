## 🔑 Quick Suffix Reference Card

| Want to find... | Look for these suffixes |
|---|---|
| **Noun (process)** | `-tion`, `-sion`, `-ment`, `-ance`, `-ence`, `-ity`, `-ness`, `-ure`, `-age` |
| **Noun (person)** | `-er`, `-or`, `-ist`, `-ant`, `-ent`, `-ee`, `-ar` |
| **Verb** | `-ize`, `-ate`, `-ify`, `-en`, or base form |
| **Adjective** | `-ive`, `-al`, `-ous`, `-ful`, `-less`, `-able`, `-ible`, `-ent`, `-ant`, `-ic`, `-ed`, `-ing` |
| **Adverb** | `-ly` (from adj.) or flat forms: hard, fast, high, late, etc. |

## 🎯 The 5 Most Common TOEIC Word Form Traps (Summary)

| Trap | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Adjective in adverb slot | "performed **successful**" | → "performed **successfully**" |
| Verb in noun slot | "the **apply** was submitted" | → "the **application** was submitted" |
| Noun in verb slot | "will **improvement** services" | → "will **improve** services" |
| `-ed` vs `-ing` adjective | "a **interesting** proposal" | → "an **interesting** proposal" |
| Person noun vs process noun | "appointed an **operation**" | → "appointed an **operator**" |

## 🧠 The Universal 4-Step Exam Strategy

> Use this for **every** word form question in Part 5:
>
> **→ Step 1: LOCATE** the blank in the sentence.
>
> **→ Step 2: SIGNAL** — What word comes before or after the blank?
> - `a / an / the / our / their` → **Noun**
> - Before a noun → **Adjective**
> - After subject (action word) → **Verb**
> - Before adj/adv, or modifying verb → **Adverb**
>
> **→ Step 3: CLASSIFY** — What class do the options belong to?
> - Quickly scan suffixes to sort: N / V / Adj / Adv
>
> **→ Step 4: MEANING** — If two options share the same class, choose by meaning:
> - Person vs. process? Positive vs. negative? Cause vs. receive feeling?

## 📝 Mixed Practice Questions

### Q1
> *"The board has requested a _____ review of the company's financial records."*

- (A) comprehend
- (B) comprehension
- **(C) comprehensive ✅**
- (D) comprehensively

> 💡 Before noun `review` → **adjective**. `comprehensive` = `-ive` suffix = adjective ✅. `comprehension` is a noun; `comprehend` is a verb; `comprehensively` is an adverb.

---

### Q2
> *"All contractors must work _____ to the terms specified in the agreement."*

- (A) strict
- (B) stricter
- (C) strictness
- **(D) strictly ✅**

> 💡 Blank modifies verb phrase `work to the terms` → **adverb** → `strictly` (`-ly`). `strict` = adjective. `strictness` = noun.

---

### Q3
> *"Ms. Park's _____ to the project was recognized at the annual awards ceremony."*

- (A) contribute
- (B) contributor
- **(C) contribution ✅**
- (D) contributing

> 💡 `Ms. Park's _____` → possessive + blank → **noun**. Need a process noun (the act of contributing) → `contribution` (`-tion`). `contributor` = person noun — doesn't fit "recognized a person's [person]" phrasing naturally.

---

### Q4
> *"The new regulation requires that all facilities be _____ inspected every six months."*

- (A) profession
- (B) professional
- **(C) professionally ✅**
- (D) professionalism

> 💡 Blank modifies past participle `inspected` (a verb form) → **adverb** → `professionally`. `professional` = adjective; `professionalism` = noun.

---

### Q5
> *"We are seeking a _____ individual to join our team as a research coordinator."*

- (A) motivation
- (B) motivate
- **(C) motivated ✅**
- (D) motivating

> 💡 Before noun `individual` → **adjective**. The person **receives** motivation → `-ed` form ✅. `motivating individual` would mean the person causes motivation in others — possible but less standard for job postings.

---

### Q6
> *"The renovation project has already had a _____ impact on staff morale."*

- (A) significance
- **(B) significant ✅**
- (C) signify
- (D) significantly

> 💡 Before noun `impact` → **adjective** → `significant` (`-ant` suffix). `significance` = noun; `signify` = verb; `significantly` = adverb (cannot modify a noun).

| Word Pair | Key Distinction |
|---|---|
| `efficient` / `efficiency` / `efficiently` | Adj / Noun / Adverb — know all three |
| `responsible` / `responsibility` / `responsibly` | Adj / Noun / Adverb |
| `significant` / `significance` / `significantly` | Adj / Noun / Adverb |
| `innovative` / `innovation` / `innovatively` | Adj / Noun / Adverb |
| `productive` / `productivity` / `productively` | Adj / Noun / Adverb |
| `competitive` / `competition` / `competitively` | Adj / Noun / Adverb |

> ✏️ **Study Method — Flash Card Rule:**
> For every new word you learn, write its **complete family**:
> → *produce → production → producer → productive → productively*
> Learn 5 forms at once → not just 1.

> ✏️ **Speed Strategy in the Exam:**
> If you can spot the signal word AND the suffix → you can answer in under **10 seconds**.
> Signal → Class → Suffix → Done. ✅
