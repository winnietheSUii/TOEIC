day-to-day operations at the individual store level.

Hannen employees to ask questions and learn about one
another's work. So far, the initiative has led to a greater
understanding among store employees of the decisions
made at the corporate level and a greater understanding
among headquarters employees on how corporate plans
are implemented.

months and conduct repeat visits annually.
